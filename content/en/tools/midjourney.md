# Midjourney

Renowned for "ceiling-level" image quality, Midjourney excels at artistic and conceptual visuals and is widely used for design inspiration.

## Strengths
Text-to-image, image-to-image, stylization, high-res upscaling; mature prompt community.

## Pricing
From ~10 USD/month; no meaningful free tier.

## Alternatives
Stable Diffusion for local control; domestic options like Tongyi Wanxiang for China users.

## Who it's for
Illustrators, designers, marketing visuals, concept artists.
