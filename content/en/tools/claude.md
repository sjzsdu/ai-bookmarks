# Claude

Anthropic's conversational model markets itself as safer, longer-context, and more natural in writing; Artifacts make interactive prototypes easy.

## Strengths
Very long context (200K+ tokens), strong at whole-PDF or codebase-level understanding; careful, less hallucinatory tone.

## Access
Direct in most regions; enterprises can call Claude through AWS Bedrock or Azure for compliance.

## vs ChatGPT
More restrained and stronger on long texts; weaker Chinese-language ecosystem than domestic models.

## Who it's for
Heavy writers, legal/finance analysts, and maintainers of large codebases.
