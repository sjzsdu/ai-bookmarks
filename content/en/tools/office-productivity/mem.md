# Mem

AI note-taking tool that auto-tags, links, and summarizes your notes without manual organization. Find information by asking natural language questions instead of browsing folders.

## Strengths
- AI auto-organization: tagging and linking
- Natural language search
- Auto-summarization
- Minimal interface focused on capture

## Pricing
Free tier with basic features, Mem X at $15/month. Accessible globally but primarily English.

## Alternatives
Notion AI, Feishu AI, Yuque AI.

## Who it's for
Anyone with lots of unorganized notes, information workers, anyone wanting AI-powered knowledge management.
