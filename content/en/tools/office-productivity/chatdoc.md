# ChatDOC

ChatDOC is an AI assistant for document reading. Upload a PDF or office file to ask questions, extract tables, summarize sections, and jump back to cited passages for verification.

## Strengths
- Conversations with PDFs and common office documents
- Summaries, information extraction, and cross-section Q&A
- Source citations linked to relevant locations
- Useful for papers, reports, contracts, and manuals

## Pricing & access
A limited free tier is available. Paid plans increase file, page, and advanced-model allowances.

## Who it's for
Researchers, students, legal professionals, consultants, and anyone who regularly reads long documents.
