Otter answers 'who writes the minutes?': it auto-joins your Zoom, Meet, or Teams calls, transcribes live with speaker labels, and delivers a summary with action items minutes after. The 300-minute free tier is enough to prove its value.

## Strengths
- Auto-join transcription for Zoom/Meet/Teams
- Live captions with speaker identification
- AI summaries and automatic action items
- Searchable, shareable meeting archive

## Pricing & access
Free 300 min/month; from ~$16.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Tongyi Tingwu (listed on this site) and Feishu Miaoji for Chinese meetings, with direct access.

## Bottom line

The English-meeting minutes ceiling; for Chinese, go domestic.
