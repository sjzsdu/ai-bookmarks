# Taskade

A productivity tool combining notes, task management, databases, and AI. AI is deeply integrated — use it anywhere to write, organize, and summarize content.

## Strengths
- AI built into every feature
- Multiple views: list, board, calendar, chart
- Team collaboration
- Rich templates

## Pricing
Free tier with basic features, Pro at $8/month. Accessible globally.

## Alternatives
Feishu (ByteDance), Notion, Wolai.

## Who it's for
Small teams, freelancers, anyone wanting one tool for notes and task management.
