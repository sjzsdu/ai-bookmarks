# Beautiful.ai

Presentation tool focused on auto-formatting — add content and it automatically arranges it beautifully. High-quality design templates produce better-looking PPTs than most tools.

## Strengths
- Smart auto-formatting
- High-quality design templates
- Team collaboration and brand templates
- Export to PPT, PDF

## Pricing
Free tier with basic features, Pro at $12/month. Accessible globally but subscription requires international payment.

## Alternatives
AiPPT, Canva (general design tool), WPS AI.

## Who it's for
Professionals wanting good-looking PPTs without formatting effort, consultants, sales teams.
