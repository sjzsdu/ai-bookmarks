Napkin nails a real pain: docs and decks lack pictures, not words. Paste a paragraph describing a process or comparison and it generates editable infographics — flows, comparison cards, step diagrams — restylable in one click, exportable as clean PNG/SVG.

## Strengths
- Text → editable infographics automatically
- Flows, comparisons, steps, hierarchies
- One-click theme and palette swaps
- PNG/SVG export

## Pricing & access
Free tier; paid unlocks more. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
No direct domestic equivalent; text-to-image tools produce art, not infographics.

## Bottom line
Small, sharp utility — document writers will stay after one use.
