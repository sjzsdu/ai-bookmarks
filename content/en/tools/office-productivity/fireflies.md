Fireflies shares Otter's transcription lane but leans into analytics: talk-time per speaker, topic tracking, flagged objections and action items — meetings as data. Full-text search across all past meetings builds a spoken knowledge base for the team.

## Strengths
- Multi-platform meeting transcription
- Speaker analytics, topic tracking, sentiment
- Semantic search across meeting history
- CRM integration (Salesforce/HubSpot)

## Pricing & access
Free tier; paid from ~$10/month, cheaper than Otter. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Tongyi Tingwu and Feishu Miaoji for Chinese meetings.

## Bottom line

For teams that treat meetings as data assets; for plain minutes, either works.
