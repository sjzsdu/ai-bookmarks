# tldraw

Open-source online whiteboard with AI features — draw a sketch, AI beautifies it into formal diagrams, wireframes, or generates frontend code. Free and open source, self-hostable.

## Strengths
- AI sketch-to-diagram conversion
- Wireframe and UI design
- Real-time collaboration
- Open source and self-hostable

## Pricing
Completely free, browser-based. Accessible globally.

## Alternatives
BoardMix (Chinese online whiteboard), Feishu Whiteboard.

## Who it's for
Product managers, designers, anyone needing to quickly sketch diagrams and workflows.
