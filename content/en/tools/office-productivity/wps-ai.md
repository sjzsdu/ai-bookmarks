WPS AI's strategy is clear: replicate what M365 Copilot does domestically at a tenth of the price. Document drafting and summarizing, spreadsheet formulas, outline-to-deck, PDF Q&A — the high-frequency office scenarios are covered. It won't match GPT-class ceilings, but it's direct-access and more than enough for daily work.

## Strengths
- Writer: generate, rewrite, summarize, polish
- Sheets: formula generation, data analysis
- Presentation: outline to deck in one click
- PDF: Q&A, summaries, conversion

## Pricing & access
Free basics; full via WPS membership (~100 RMB/year tier). Direct access.

## Compared
M365 Copilot for enterprises with budget; WPS AI is the pragmatic pick for individuals and SMEs.

## Bottom line
Affordable AI catch-up from the national office suite — practical and adequate.
