M365 Copilot is the 'official answer' to AI office work: not another web chat, but capability embedded in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook — draft in Word, analyze data in Excel with a sentence, summarize threads in Outlook. The cost: $30/user/month, worthwhile mainly for enterprises.

## Strengths
- Word: draft, rewrite, summarize
- Excel: natural-language analysis and visualization
- PowerPoint: decks from documents
- Outlook/Teams: mail and meeting summaries
- Enterprise data isolation, no training on your content

## Pricing & access
$30/user/month on top of M365. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
WPS AI and Feishu partners domestically, each ecosystem-bound.

## Bottom line
A standard upgrade for Microsoft-heavy enterprises; individuals should use free Copilot.
