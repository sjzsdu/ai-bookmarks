# Qwen

Alibaba's AI chat model, free and direct-access in China. Strong multimodal capabilities — understands images and documents. Integrated with the Alibaba Cloud ecosystem.

## Strengths
- Multimodal chat (text, image, document understanding)
- Strong Chinese, free to use
- Integrated with Alibaba Cloud (Tongyi Wanxiang, Lingma, etc.)
- Open-source models for local deployment

## Pricing & access
Direct access in China, free, Alibaba Cloud account required.

## Alternatives
DeepSeek (stronger reasoning), Kimi (longer context), ERNIE Bot (Baidu), Doubao (ByteDance).

## Who it's for
Multimodal tasks, Alibaba Cloud users, anyone wanting easy, direct access in China.
