# Kimi

Moonshot AI's chat model, built around ultra-long context (200K+ characters). Ideal for processing long documents, summaries, and analysis.

## Strengths
- Ultra-long context — reads entire books or long reports
- Document summarization, Q&A, analysis
- Web search for real-time information
- Direct access in China, free to use

## Pricing & access
Direct access in China, free, phone registration required.

## Alternatives
DeepSeek (stronger reasoning), Tongyi Wanxiang (Alibaba), ERNIE Bot (Baidu).

## Who it's for
Researchers, students, analysts working with long documents who want easy, direct access.
