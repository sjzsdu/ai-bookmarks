Claude is especially good as a patient editor for long notes, transcripts, and drafts. Ask it to map the material, then challenge the details. Fluent prose is not evidence—check contracts, financial claims, and source material.

Anthropic's conversational model — safer, longer-context, more natural writing. Artifacts make interactive prototypes easy.

## Strengths
- Very long context (200K+ tokens) for whole-PDF or codebase understanding
- Careful, less hallucinatory tone
- Artifacts for interactive prototypes
- Strong coding ability

## Pricing & access
Free tier available; Pro ~20 USD/month. Available globally; enterprise via AWS Bedrock or Azure.

## Alternatives
ChatGPT (broader features), Gemini (search integration), DeepSeek (free, strong Chinese).

## Who it's for
Heavy writers, legal/finance analysts, maintainers of large codebases.
