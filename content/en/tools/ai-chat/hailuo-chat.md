From MiniMax, Hailuo AI's claim to fame is voice: its Chinese speech interaction is among the most natural and human-feeling of domestic assistants — better for spoken practice and companion chat than typing. Text capability rides on MiniMax models, adequate for daily Q&A and writing.

## Strengths
- Human-like voice chat with emotional expression
- Q&A, writing, document understanding
- Links into the Hailuo video ecosystem
- Multi-device sync

## Pricing & access
Free, direct access.

## Compared
Voice → Hailuo, long context → Kimi, general → Doubao, reasoning → DeepSeek.

## Bottom line
Voice is the moat; the text side is solid but unremarkable.
