# ERNIE Bot

Baidu's Chinese large model (ERNIE), accessible in China without VPN, integrated with Baidu search, netdisk, and docs. Natural Chinese expression.

## Strengths
- Chat, writing, PPT generation, image/video generation
- Natural Chinese with Baidu search integration
- Plugin ecosystem (search, netdisk, docs)
- Enterprise access via Baidu Intelligent Cloud

## Pricing & access
Direct access in China, free, phone registration. Enterprise via Baidu Intelligent Cloud.

## Alternatives
DeepSeek (stronger reasoning), Qwen (better multimodal), Kimi (longer context), Doubao (ByteDance).

## Who it's for
Users in China wanting easy access, Baidu ecosystem enterprises, Chinese content production teams.
