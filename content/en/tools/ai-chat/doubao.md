Doubao is handy for small everyday jobs—polishing copy, brainstorming short-video titles, or asking about an image. Its value is ease and Chinese fluency, not rigorous research; use primary sources when traceability matters.

ByteDance's AI chat assistant, free and direct-access in China. Well-integrated with Douyin, Feishu, and other ByteDance products.

## Strengths
- Natural Chinese conversation for daily use
- Douyin ecosystem integration (video understanding, content creation)
- Multimodal support (image, voice)
- Direct access in China, free

## Pricing & access
Direct access in China, free, phone registration required.

## Alternatives
DeepSeek (stronger reasoning), Qwen (better multimodal), Kimi (longer context).

## Who it's for
Douyin ecosystem users, daily AI assistant needs, anyone wanting easy access in China.
