Character.AI turns chatting into a character experience: millions of community-built personas — historical figures, anime characters, originals — each with their own voice. You can build your own and share it. It's entertainment and companionship, not fact lookup, but nobody matches its immersion.

## Strengths
- Huge categorized character library
- Character editor with persona, greetings, examples
- Voice conversations
- Group chat with multiple characters

## Pricing & access
Free; c.ai+ ~$9.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Xingye (MiniMax) and Maoxiang (ByteDance) for Chinese roleplay with direct access.

## Bottom line
Entertainment first — expecting factual answers is a category error.
