Mistral is Europe's AI flagship and Le Chat its consumer product. It's fast — streaming responses noticeably quicker than most rivals — and compliant, as a European company with EU-friendly data handling. Web search, image generation, and a code interpreter are all built in: a complete, slightly-less-powerful ChatGPT alternative.

## Strengths
- Mistral models with exceptional response speed
- Web search, news, image generation, code interpreter
- Enterprise data never used for training
- Self-hosting options for developers

## Pricing & access
Free tier; Pro from ~$14.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
Faster and cheaper than ChatGPT, simpler than self-hosting; Chinese capability is its weak spot.

## Bottom line
The compliance-friendly ChatGPT substitute; Chinese-heavy users should prefer domestic options.
