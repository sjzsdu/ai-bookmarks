# DeepSeek

DeepSeek's AI chat model, known for being free and capable. Strong Chinese, supports deep thinking mode, top-tier reasoning among Chinese models.

## Strengths
- Strong Chinese chat, writing, and coding
- Deep thinking mode (slower, deeper reasoning)
- Open-source models for local deployment
- Completely free

## Pricing & access
Direct access in China, completely free, phone registration. API pay-as-you-go at low prices.

## Alternatives
Tongyi Wanxiang (Alibaba), Kimi (Moonshot), ERNIE Bot (Baidu), Doubao (ByteDance).

## Who it's for
Nearly everyone — free, easy to use, one of the best AI chat entry points in China right now.
