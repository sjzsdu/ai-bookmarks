# Gemini

Google's AI chat model with native multimodal understanding — can process text, images, video, and audio directly. Deep Google Search integration for real-time information.

## Strengths
- Native multimodal (text, image, video, audio)
- Google Search integration for real-time info
- Very long context (up to 2M tokens)
- Integrated with Google Workspace

## Pricing & access
Free tier available; Advanced plan ~20 USD/month. Available globally.

## Alternatives
ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), DeepSeek (open-source, strong Chinese).

## Who it's for
Heavy Google Workspace users, multimodal tasks, English content creators.
