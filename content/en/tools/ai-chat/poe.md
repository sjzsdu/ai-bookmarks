# Poe

Quora's AI aggregator — one platform for multiple AI models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Llama, etc.). Switch between models seamlessly.

## Strengths
- Multi-model aggregation in one platform
- Create and share custom bots
- Image generation integration (DALL-E, Stable Diffusion)
- Monthly subscription with per-model credits

## Pricing & access
Limited free tier; subscription ~20 USD/month. Available globally.

## Alternatives
Direct API access to individual models (cheaper at scale), ChatGPT Plus (single model, deeper features).

## Who it's for
AI enthusiasts wanting to compare models, global users who want a single entry point.
