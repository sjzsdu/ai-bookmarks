Yuanbao's edge is ecosystem: it's one of the few AI assistants that can search WeChat official-account articles, a Chinese content source nobody else can index. Hunyuan and DeepSeek dual engines cover daily Q&A and writing, completely free.

## Strengths
- WeChat official-account search (exclusive)
- Hunyuan + DeepSeek-R1 switchable models
- Document upload and image understanding
- Usable from WeChat mini-programs

## Pricing & access
Free, direct access in China.

## Compared
Doubao (slightly stronger models), Kimi (longer context); Yuanbao wins on ecosystem and WeChat search.

## Bottom line
WeChat articles are among the best content pools on the Chinese web — that alone earns it a slot.
