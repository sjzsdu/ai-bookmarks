# ChatGPT

OpenAI's conversational model is the most recognized AI entry point worldwide, strong at writing, coding, translation and brainstorming.

## What it's good at
Long-form writing, code generation, data analysis, and (on paid tiers) web search.

## Pricing & access
Free tier plus a ~20 USD/month Plus plan; enterprise and API pricing scale with usage. Available in most regions without a VPN.

## Western alternatives
Claude (Anthropic) for careful writing, Gemini (Google) for search integration, and open models (Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek) for self-hosting.

## Who it's for
Students, operators, developers and researchers — nearly universal, with the main limit being cost at scale.
