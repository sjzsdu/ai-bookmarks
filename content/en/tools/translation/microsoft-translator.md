Microsoft Translator is the most underrated free option: Google-class quality, entirely free, embedded in Edge and Office. Full-page translation works out of the box, and Teams' live multi-language captions are nearly irreplaceable for global collaboration. Without a proxy, it's the ready substitute for Google.

## Strengths
- Text, page, document, and speech translation
- Native in Edge/Office
- Teams live multi-language captions
- Free Azure Translator API tier

## Pricing & access
Free; usable via Bing/Edge.

## Compared
Google (proxy needed), DeepL (quality), Microsoft (reachability + ecosystem).

## Bottom line
The default translator inside Microsoft's world and the mainstay without a proxy.
