From Northeastern University's NLP lab, Niutrans is a veteran Chinese MT engine: 304 languages lead the domestic field, and vertical customization — training legal, medical, or finance engines on domain corpora — is its signature. The API's free quota makes integration cheap.

## Strengths
- 304-language MT
- Vertical-domain engine customization
- API, plugins, private deployment
- Translation-memory support

## Pricing & access
Monthly free quota, then per-character. Direct access.

## Compared
Baidu/Youdao (consumer experience), Niutrans (languages and customization) — B-end integration favors Niutrans.

## Bottom line
The professional squad among Chinese engines, serving business first.
