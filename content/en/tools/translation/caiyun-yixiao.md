Caiyun Xiaoyi pioneered the bilingual side-by-side pattern: translated pages show原文 and译文 paragraph by paragraph, so reading foreign articles doubles as language study. Its in-house EN↔ZH engine has long been respected domestically, and LLM translation has been added.

## Strengths
- Bilingual web translation
- Bilingual PDF/Word output
- Voice simultaneous mode
- Extensions across browsers

## Pricing & access
Generous free tier; membership for documents. Direct access.

## Compared
Immersive Translate (more engines, bigger community), Caiyun (steady in-house quality) — pick one.

## Bottom line
A classic for bilingual reading.
