For four decades Trados has been the professional translation standard: its core isn't machine translation but translation memory — every translated sentence is stored and reused via exact or fuzzy matches. Large projects live on this consistency engine. For professionals it's a resume skill; for everyone else, overkill.

## Strengths
- Translation memory reuse
- Termbase consistency control
- Multi-user project management
- MT plug-ins (DeepL/LLM)

## Pricing & access
Professional subscription. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
YiCAT (domestic cloud CAT), OmegaT (free).

## Bottom line
Localization infrastructure that doesn't aim at light users.
