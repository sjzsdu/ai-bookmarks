Baidu Translate covers the widest language range among domestic services: 200+ languages, often the only good option for Southeast Asian or Middle Eastern languages. Camera translation is a travel essential. In the LLM era its per-sentence quality no longer leads, but coverage and tooling remain strong.

## Strengths
- 200+ language pairs
- Camera, voice, and document modes
- Translation API and open platform
- Desktop hover-lookup and screenshot tools

## Pricing & access
Free with API quota. Direct access.

## Compared
Google Translate (proxy needed), Youdao (deeper lookup), Baidu (widest coverage).

## Bottom line
Its ground is minor languages and complete tooling.
