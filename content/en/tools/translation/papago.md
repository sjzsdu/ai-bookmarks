Papago, from Naver, stands on Korean quality: honorifics, contractions, and internet slang handled clearly better than general translators — the default for Korea travel and Korean content.

## Getting started

Download the Korean offline pack on mobile; use camera mode for sites and products; paste text for subtitles and chats.

## What you can actually do

- Korean-specialized text/camera/voice translation
- Japanese, Chinese, English, SE Asian pairs
- Naver dictionary and encyclopedia integration

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Mediocre beyond Korean; long formal documents still need humans. Generally accessible in China with occasional instability.

## Alternatives

DeepL and Google for Japanese, Youdao for Chinese, LLMs for polishing formal text.
