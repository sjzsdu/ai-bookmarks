# Naver Papago

Built by Naver (Korea's biggest search engine), Papago crushes Korean translation in a way no competitor matches. Supports English, Japanese, Chinese, and Korean. If you deal with Korean content regularly, this is the tool to use.

## Strengths
- Korean translation accuracy is best-in-class
- Supports EN/JA/ZH/KO
- Image translation, document translation, web page translation
- Mobile apps and browser extension

## Pricing & Access
- Completely free
- Accessible from China
- iOS and Android apps available

## Alternatives
- Google Translate (Korean)
- DeepL (Korean support is decent)
- Naver Dictionary

## Who it's for
Korean language learners, anyone doing business with Korea, K-drama fans who want quick translations.
