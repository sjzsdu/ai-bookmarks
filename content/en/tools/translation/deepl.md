# DeepL Translator

The translation quality benchmark. Built by a German team using neural networks, DeepL consistently outperforms Google Translate for European language pairs (English-German-French-Spanish). Free tier gives you 500K characters per month.

## Strengths
- 30+ languages including CJK
- More natural output than competitors
- Document translation (Word/PPT/PDF)
- Browser extension and desktop apps

## Pricing & Access
- Free: 500K characters/month, 5 document translations
- Pro: $9/month, unlimited characters, offline mode
- Credit card and PayPal accepted

## Alternatives
- Google Translate
- Microsoft Translator
- Lingvanex

## Who it's for
Anyone who translates documents regularly and cares about output quality — academics, content creators, international teams.
