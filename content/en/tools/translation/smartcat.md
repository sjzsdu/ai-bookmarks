# Smartcat

Enterprise-grade translation and localization platform that blends AI and human translation workflows. Includes translation memory, glossary management, and quality checks. Built for serious, large-scale translation projects.

## Strengths
- AI + human translation workflow
- Translation memory and glossary
- Quality checks and consistency validation
- Integrates with Figma, GitHub, and more

## Pricing & Access
- Free: basic features, 3 users
- Enterprise: $99/month, more users and advanced features
- Credit card accepted
- May require VPN from China

## Alternatives
- Phrase (formerly Memsource)
- Lokalise
- Smartling

## Who it's for
Enterprises needing professional translation services, teams managing large localization projects.
