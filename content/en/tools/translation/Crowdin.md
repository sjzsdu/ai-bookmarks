# Crowdin

The go-to platform for managing localization projects. Connects to GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket, auto-generates translation tasks, and lets teams collaborate on translation, review, and publishing. Built for App/website/game multilingual localization.

## Strengths
- GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket integration
- 50+ file format support
- Translation memory and glossary
- Automated workflows and approval processes

## Pricing & Access
- Free: 1 project, 3 collaborators
- Team: $40/month, more projects and collaborators
- Open source projects get enterprise features free
- Credit card accepted

## Alternatives
- Transifex
- Lokalise
- Phrase

## Who it's for
Development teams and product managers doing app/website multilingual localization.
