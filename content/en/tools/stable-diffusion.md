# Stable Diffusion

A fully open text-to-image family (SD1.5, SDXL, fine-tunes) whose biggest advantage is local deployment and full control.

## Why users love it
Open weights, offline local run, huge plugin/model ecosystem (LoRA, ControlNet), no paywall.

## Capabilities
Text/image-to-image, inpainting, style training (LoRA).

## Trade-off
Needs GPU power and tinkering; quality tuning is more effort than Midjourney.

## Who it's for
Privacy/control-focused teams, batch asset production, custom-style training.
