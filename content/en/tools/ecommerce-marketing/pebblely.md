Pebblely's logic is minimal: upload one product photo and receive 40+ AI-generated scenes — marble counters, outdoor picnics, holiday themes — then download the keepers. Forty free images a month lets small sellers start at zero, and batch mode keeps brand style uniform.

## Strengths
- One-click 40+ scene generation
- Custom scene prompts
- Batch processing with brand style
- 4K export on paid tiers

## Pricing & access
40 free/month; ~$19/month to unlock. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Meitu and Gaoding scene features domestically; Pebblely is smoother for multi-scene.

## Bottom line
Light, cheap, brainless scene images — small-seller friendly.
