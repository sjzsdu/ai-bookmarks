Surfer turns 'how do I rank?' into a data question: enter a keyword and it analyzes the top results — length, structure, term usage, NLP vocabulary — into a content brief, then scores your draft live as you write. Pair with AI first drafts and human polish: the current standard for English content SEO.

## Strengths
- SERP analysis and content briefs
- Real-time Content Score
- Surfer AI article generation
- Keyword clustering and planning

## Pricing & access

A productivity tool for English content SEO; the math depends on monetization.
