HubSpot won the inbound-marketing era and now threads AI (Breeze) through everything: email drafting, interaction summaries, deal scoring, chatbots. The free CRM has a low floor with paid tiers as you grow. For scaling companies wanting marketing, sales, and service in one system, it's among the most complete.

## Strengths
- CRM + marketing + sales + service in one
- Breeze AI for content and insights
- Automation workflows and lead scoring
- Huge integration ecosystem

## Pricing & access
Free CRM; tiered upgrades. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Chinese CRMs differ locally; HubSpot is smooth for going global.

## Bottom line
The first-choice marketing hub for international-facing teams.
