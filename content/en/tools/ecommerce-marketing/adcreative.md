AdCreative.ai targets the creative layer of performance ads: upload a product, batch-generate creatives, and get a 'conversion score' per image — data-first creative selection is its whole differentiator.

## Getting started

Set up the brand kit (logo, palette, fonts), then batch-generate from product photos. Sort by score and A/B test the top few.

## What you can actually do

- Creative generation across sizes and placements
- Conversion scoring for click potential
- Ad-account integration for direct pushes

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Scores are predictions, not promises — keep real A/B tests. Subscriptions aren't cheap; compute whether your creative volume justifies it.

## Alternatives

Canva for pure design, Flair or Pebblely for product scenes, Jasper or Writesonic for copy.
