# AdCreative.ai

Skip the designer for ad creatives. AI generates ad images and video assets from your product info. Supports Facebook, Google, TikTok and other platforms. Good enough for testing, but final polish still needs a human touch.

## Strengths
- AI ad image generation
- Video ad assets
- Auto-matches copy to creative
- Multi-platform adaptation (FB/Google/TikTok)

## Pricing & Access
- Basic: $29/month, 10 downloads/month
- Credit card required
- Requires VPN from China
- Free trial available

## Who it's for
E-commerce sellers and marketing teams needing lots of ad creatives on a budget.
