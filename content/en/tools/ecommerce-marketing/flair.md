Flair attacks the cost of lifestyle photography: skip the studio and props, and compose scenes by drag — upload the product, place AI-generated props and backdrops, tune lighting, export marketing-grade shots. Seasonal variants are just re-dressed scenes.

## Strengths
- Drag-and-drop scene building
- Perspective- and light-aware AI props
- Category template library
- Team collaboration and brand assets

## Pricing & access
Free tier; from ~$10/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Meitu's product-scene features partially cover this; no mature domestic suite.

## Bottom line
A virtual photo studio for DTC brands and small sellers.
