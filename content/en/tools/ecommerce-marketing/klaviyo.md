# Klaviyo

The email marketing choice for cross-border e-commerce. AI optimizes send times, personalizes content, and predicts user behavior. Integrates beautifully with Shopify — auto-trigger cart abandonment emails, welcome flows, and more.

## Strengths
- AI smart send time optimization
- Personalized email content
- User churn prediction
- Automated marketing flows

## Pricing & Access
- Free: 250 contacts
- Paid: $20/month starting price
- Credit card required
- Requires VPN from China

Cross-border e-commerce sellers, Shopify/independent store email marketing needs.
