Alimama is the ad hub for Taobao-ecosystem merchants: Wanxiang unifies Zhitongche and Yinlimofang into smart campaigns where AI handles audience expansion, bidding, and creative selection, with data looping back.

## Getting started

Run smart campaigns to accumulate data on new stores; keep manual plans for hero SKUs. Review AI creatives for platform compliance before launch.

## What you can actually do

- Smart campaigns driven by goals and budget
- Audience management via Damodisk (DMP)
- AI creative: image/copy generation and selection

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Media spend dwarfs tool costs — the learning curve is the platform logic itself. Follow AIGC labeling rules for generated creatives.

## Alternatives

JD sellers use Yanxi and Jingzhuntong, Douyin buyers use Qianchuan, Meitu Design Studio for asset prep.
