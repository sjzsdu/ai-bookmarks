Photoroom compresses product-photo workflows onto a phone: shoot an item, and AI cuts it out, generates a lighting-matched background, and sizes it for listing. Batch mode processes an entire SKU library consistently. For small sellers it replaces a chunk of retouching outsourcing.

## Strengths
- AI cutout and background replacement
- AI scene backgrounds matched to product light
- Batch processing with unified templates
- Platform-specific sizing

## Pricing & access
Free basics; Pro subscription. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Gaoding and Meitu's e-commerce tools; Photoroom leads on cutout quality.

## Bottom line
A picture production line for small e-commerce at high value.
