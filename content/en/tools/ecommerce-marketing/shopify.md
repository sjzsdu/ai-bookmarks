Shopify is the de-facto standard for independent e-commerce: store, inventory, payments, shipping, and marketing in one, now with AI (Shopify Magic) woven through product copy, email, and support. It's not an 'AI tool' — it's the most complete AI-enabled commerce base.

## Getting started

Pick a theme, frame the store, and let Magic draft product descriptions for manual polish. Cart-recovery and welcome flows turn on out of the box.

## What you can actually do

- Store building: theme, domain, payments
- AI copy: descriptions, SEO, email drafts
- App store for reviews, support, and ads

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Monthly fees plus transaction cuts need real math; deep customization takes developers. China access and settlement need extra arrangements.

## Alternatives

Squarespace for lighter sites, Youzan/WeShop for domestic ecosystems, Photoroom and Pebblely for product imagery.
