# Arc Browser

A browser that reinvented tabs, bookmarks, and search — with AI built into the command bar.

## Strengths
- Spaces for organizing tabs by project or context
- Built-in AI search and page summarization
- Command-bar navigation (⌘T, ⌘L) for power users
- Auto-archives inactive tabs to reduce clutter

## Pricing & access
Free. Available on Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android. AI features may require VPN outside the US.

## Alternatives
Chrome + extensions (more familiar), Edge + Copilot (AI built-in).

## Who it's for
People with 50+ tabs open who want a cleaner, keyboard-driven browsing experience.
