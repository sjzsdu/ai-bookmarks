# Metaso

One of the best Chinese AI search engines — high-quality answers in Chinese, clean formatting.

## Strengths
- Excellent Chinese language understanding and search quality
- Structured answers with citations
- Academic search mode for research papers
- Completely free with no limits

## Pricing & access
Free, direct access from China, no VPN needed. Phone number registration required.

## Alternatives
TianGong AI Search (tiangong.cn), Baidu AI Search.

## Who it's for
Chinese-speaking users doing research, academic work, or general web searches.
