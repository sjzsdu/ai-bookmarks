Genspark wants to reinvent results: ask a comparison question and you get a freshly built Sparkpage — structured, tabular, sourced — instead of ten blue links. Agents are built in too: price checks, bookings, even phone calls can be delegated. Radical but clearly pointed.

## Strengths
- Custom Sparkpage generation per query
- Multi-model orchestration (GPT/Claude/in-house)
- Agents for multi-step tasks
- Handles Chinese queries

## Pricing & access
Daily free quota; from ~$24.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
Perplexity is more mature; Genspark is more aggressive. Tiangong and Metaso are domestic stand-ins.

## Bottom line

A proving ground for search's future — worth trying for research queries.
