Perplexity defined the AI-search category: every answer cites its sources, and Pro Search cross-checks across multiple rounds before answering. English coverage and citation discipline remain the industry benchmark; the Comet browser extends search into browsing.

## Getting started

Ask directly on the free tier; use Pro Search for complex questions and steer follow-ups. Save good sessions as Threads to reuse.

## What you can actually do

- Cited answers linking every claim
- Pro Search multi-round research
- Spaces for project knowledge and search

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Chinese content coverage trails domestic tools. Citations occasionally mis-attribute — open the source for load-bearing claims. Free Pro queries are capped daily.

## Alternatives

Metaso or Tiangong for direct China access, Phind and Devv for developers, You.com for model choice.
