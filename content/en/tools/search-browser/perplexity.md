# Perplexity AI

The closest thing to asking a knowledgeable friend a question and getting a sourced answer back.

## Strengths
- Synthesizes search results into a complete answer with per-sentence citations
- Supports image and file search
- Pro plan unlocks GPT-4, Claude, and other advanced models
- Copilot feature asks follow-up questions to refine results

## Pricing & access
Free tier includes 200 Pro searches daily. Pro plan at $20/month adds more advanced models and file uploads. Available globally.

## Alternatives
Phind (developer-focused), You.com (customizable search).

## Who it's for
Researchers, writers, analysts, and anyone tired of clicking through pages of search results.
