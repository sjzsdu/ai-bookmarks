Opera One makes AI a native browser feature: the Aria sidebar summarizes the current page, answers questions, and keeps context — no extensions, no window switching. With mature tab islands and vertical tabs, it's among the most practical takes on the 'AI-native browser', and it's free.

## Strengths
- Aria assistant (chat, summaries, generation)
- Tab islands and vertical tabs
- Built-in VPN and ad blocking
- Full platform coverage including Linux

## Pricing & access

Free; the site itself may need a proxy in China.

## Compared
Arc (design), Edge (Copilot), Brave (privacy); Aria has the most generous free quota.

## Bottom line

The sensible pick when you want a new browser with AI included.
