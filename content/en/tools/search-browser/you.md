# You.com

Privacy-conscious AI search that lets you pick your model and opt out of tracking.

## Strengths
- Model switching: GPT-4, Claude, and more
- Privacy mode — no search history tracking
- Chat mode for follow-up questions
- Search results with citations

## Pricing & access
Free tier with basic search. Pro at $15/month for advanced models and unlimited search. Global access.

## Alternatives
Perplexity (more popular), Brave Search (privacy-focused, no AI).

## Who it's for
Users who want AI search without surveillance and prefer choosing their own model.
