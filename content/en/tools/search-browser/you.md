You.com differentiates on openness: choose the model behind your answers, from GPT to open Llama, with a no-tracking privacy stance. Not the strongest AI search, but the most control.

## Getting started

Set your preferred default model in settings; use it as a private daily search substitute; YouAgent suits light reasoning questions.

## What you can actually do

- Model switching for the same query
- Private, untracked search and chat
- Custom source preferences

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Free tier limits model choices and volume. Chinese content is weak. Pro unlocks models that shift with the market.

## Alternatives

Perplexity for citation rigor, Phind for developers, Metaso for direct China access.
