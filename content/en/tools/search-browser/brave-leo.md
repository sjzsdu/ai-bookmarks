Brave's stance is in the name: ads and trackers blocked by default, and even its AI promises not to keep your conversations. Leo supports multiple models (Claude, Llama, Mistral) — a rare choice for users who don't want an AI company reading their prompts. Brave Search runs its own index, independent of Google.

## Strengths
- Default ad/tracker blocking
- Leo assistant (multi-model, no stored chats)
- Brave Search with an independent index
- Chromium core, Chrome extensions work

## Pricing & access

Browser free; Leo premium ~$15/month. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
Firefox (privacy, no AI), Opera (more integrated AI); Brave's privacy promise is the strongest.

## Bottom line

Privacy is the only selling point — and it's enough.
