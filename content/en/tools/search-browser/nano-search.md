Nano AI Search (formerly 360 AI Search) is one of China's highest-traffic AI search engines: free, direct access, wired into the 360 browser ecosystem. Its hook is multi-model dispatch — the same question answered by DeepSeek and other models on demand. Page, video, and document Q&A round it out.

## Strengths
- AI search with model switching
- Page/video/document Q&A
- Slow-thinking mode for deep reasoning
- Mobile and browser integration

## Pricing & access

Free, direct access.

## Compared
Metaso (academic), Tiangong (snappy), Nano (ecosystem) — pick by taste.

## Bottom line

The safe default for Chinese AI search.
