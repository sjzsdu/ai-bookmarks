Kagi proves another path exists: search as a paid utility — clean, ad-free, untracked, untainted by SEO farms. Its signature feature is domain weighting: permanently demote Pinterest, boost personal blogs, and your results reshaped to your taste. A purist's paradise.

## Strengths
- Ad-free, tracker-free subscription search
- Domain-level personal ranking
- Kagi AI assistant and focused search
- Strong indexing of quality indie sites

## Pricing & access

From ~$5/month. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
DuckDuckGo is the free privacy option with average quality; Kagi's quality justifies the price.

## Bottom line

A one-of-a-kind paid search — undervalued for heavy users.
