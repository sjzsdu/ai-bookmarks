Exa isn't a search engine for humans; it's one for AI: neural retrieval matches pages by meaning, and results ship with cleaned full text ready for model context. Agents and RAG pipelines want exactly this 'retrieval as raw material' API, and Exa leads the niche.

## Strengths
- Neural and keyword search modes
- Find-similar page discovery
- Cleaned full-text content in results
- Category, domain, and date filters

## Pricing & access

Generous free tier, then per-request billing. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
Tavily (direct rival), Serper (Google-results API); Exa leads on semantic retrieval.

## Bottom line

Retrieval infrastructure for developers — invisible to everyone else.
