Veo is Google DeepMind's video model, sitting alongside Sora and Kling in the first tier. Two things stand out: physical realism (people, fluids, and lighting rarely break) and native audio — videos come with dialogue and sound effects, no post-production needed.

## Strengths
- Text-to-video and image-to-video, up to 4K depending on version
- Native synchronized dialogue and ambient audio
- Camera language (moves, depth, cuts) directed via text
- Available in Gemini, the Flow tool, and the Vertex API

## Pricing & access
Included with Gemini (~$20/month); API billed per second on Vertex AI. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Kling, Hailuo, and Jimeng — direct access, RMB billing, equally first-tier quality with friendlier Chinese prompting.

## Bottom line
A natural pick inside the Google ecosystem; otherwise compare free tiers with Kling before subscribing.
