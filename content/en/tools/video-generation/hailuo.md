# Hailuo AI

MiniMax's AI video generator — top-tier quality among Chinese tools. Text-to-video and image-to-video with natural motion and varied camera language.

## Strengths
- Text-to-video and image-to-video
- Natural motion and rich camera movements
- Fast generation with free trial credits

## Pricing & access
Free tier available; pay-as-you-go. Accessible globally and directly in China.

## Alternatives
Kling (stronger on long videos), Runway (pro-grade editing), Pika (lightweight and fast).

## Who it's for
Short-video creators, marketing teams, anyone needing quick video clips from text or images.
