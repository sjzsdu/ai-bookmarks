Viggle does one specific thing: give it a character image plus a reference video, and the character performs the moves — dancing, fighting, emoting — with surprisingly natural tracking. It's not a general video generator, but in 'make my character move' there is no real competitor.

## Strengths
- Image + reference video → motion transfer
- Multi-character scenes and green-screen output
- Simple web and Discord entry points

## Pricing & access
Free quota; paid from ~$9.9/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Jimeng and Kling offer partial motion-imitation features with direct access.

## Bottom line
A productivity tool for playful content — judge it on fun and shareability, not fidelity.
