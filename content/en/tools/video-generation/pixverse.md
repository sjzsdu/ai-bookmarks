# PixVerse

Fast and fun AI video generation with plenty of style templates — anime, realistic, 3D — making short clips from text or images effortless.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-video
- Multiple style templates
- Motion control and effects overlay

## Pricing & access
Limited free tier; paid plans from 8 USD/month. Generally accessible directly in most regions.

## Alternatives
Pika (lighter, faster), Runway (more pro features), Kling (stronger long videos).

## Who it's for
Short-video creators, social media operators, anyone wanting to quickly try AI video.
