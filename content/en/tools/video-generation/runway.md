# Runway

The benchmark in AI video — its Gen models made text-to-video real. Comprehensive pipeline from generation to editing to effects.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-video, video repair, object erase, motion brush, upscaling
- Rich AI effects library (100+ effects)
- Professional-grade video editing workflow

## Pricing & access
Limited free tier; paid plans from 12 USD/month. Available globally via web app.

## Alternatives
Kling (stronger long videos, China), Pika (lighter, faster), Luma (better 3D consistency).

## Who it's for
Ad production, film pre-vis, independent creators needing pro-grade AI video editing.
