CapCut is less an 'AI video generator' than the most-used AI-assisted editor on the planet: script-to-video turns copy into a finished cut with visuals, voiceover, and subtitles, while auto captions, filler removal, and smart cutout clear out the busywork. For short-form creators, it is the production line.

## Strengths
- Script-to-video in one click
- Speech recognition, TTS, auto subtitles
- AI avatars, effects, and cutout
- Huge, fast-moving template library

## Pricing & access
Free basics; Pro (~$8/mo) unlocks avatars and premium assets. Direct access in China.

## Alternatives
Descript for podcast-style editing; CapCut still wins on mobile and templates.

## Caveat
Check asset licensing before commercial publication.
