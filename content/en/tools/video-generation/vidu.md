# Vidu

Shengshu AI's video generation model, focused on multi-subject consistency and long-video coherence. A strong contender among Chinese video AI tools.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-video
- Multi-subject consistency — characters stay stable throughout
- Good long-video coherence for narrative content

## Pricing & access
Direct access in China, free tier available, RMB pay-as-you-go.

## Alternatives
Kling (better motion naturalness), Hailuo AI (faster), Runway (pro-grade, global).

## Who it's for
Creators making narrative videos, short dramas, and character-driven content.
