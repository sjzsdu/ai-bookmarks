Most AI video tools solve a shot; LTX Studio solves a story. Feed it a script and it generates a storyboard with consistent characters and scenes, then lets you tune camera and content per shot on a timeline before stitching the cut. Per-frame quality isn't the absolute ceiling, but cross-shot consistency is unmatched.

## Strengths
- Script-to-storyboard with automatic scene breakdown
- Character and scene consistency across shots
- Timeline editor with per-shot camera and duration control
- Open LTX Video backbone with fast generation

## Pricing & access
Monthly free quota; paid from ~$15/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
No true narrative equivalent in China; Kling and Hailuo are better for single shots.

## Bottom line
The most complete option if you treat AI video as storytelling rather than clip generation.
