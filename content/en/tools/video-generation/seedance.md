Jimeng is ByteDance's AI creation platform. Its video workflow uses Seedance models and connects with Seedream image generation, making it easy to turn visual concepts into motion content.

## Strengths
- Text-to-video and image-to-video generation
- Controls for camera movement, aspect ratio, and visual style
- Connected image and video creation workflow
- Strong Chinese prompt understanding

## Pricing & access
Free trial credits are available. Advanced features or additional generations may require payment; current allowances are shown in the product.

## Who it's for
Short-video creators, ecommerce teams, advertisers, and anyone producing motion assets for Chinese audiences.
