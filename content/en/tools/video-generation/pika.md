# Pika

Went viral for being fun, fast, and easy — turning ideas into short clips quickly. Low barrier to entry, fast output.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-video, video stylization, local animation, sound effects
- Simple and fast to learn
- Great for social media shorts and meme-style animations

## Pricing & access
Free tier available; paid plans from 8 USD/month. Generally accessible directly in most regions.

## Alternatives
Kling (better long videos), Runway (pro-grade), PixVerse (more style templates).

## Who it's for
Short-video creators, social media operators, individuals doing light visual experiments.
