Tencent open-sourced its video model: 13B-parameter HunyuanVideo sits at the top of open-source quality, with downloadable and commercially usable weights. For anyone with hardware, it's the path to first-tier quality for free; for developers, open weights mean fine-tuning, integration, and redistribution.

## Strengths
- Text-to-video and image-to-video
- Dual-stream to single-stream Transformer with solid motion coherence
- Pairs with HunyuanImage and Hunyuan3D for a full open stack
- Mature community quantizations and ComfyUI support

## Pricing & access
Free and open; the official web app is directly accessible from China. Local deployment needs a large-VRAM GPU.

## Alternatives
Kling and Hailuo cloud services are easier, equally good, pay-per-use.

## Bottom line
Built for technical teams and GPU owners; casual users should stick to the hosted web version.
