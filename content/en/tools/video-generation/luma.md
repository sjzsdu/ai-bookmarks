# Luma Dream Machine

Luma AI's video generator, excelling at natural camera movement and 3D consistency. Product showcases and spatial tours look better here than in most alternatives.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-video
- Smooth, natural camera movement
- Strong 3D spatial consistency

## Pricing & access
Free tier available; paid plans from 9.99 USD/month. Generally accessible directly in most regions.

## Alternatives
Kling (better for long videos in China), Runway (pro-grade editing), Pika (faster, lighter).

## Who it's for
Product showcase creators, spatial video makers, anyone needing natural camera movement.
