# Sora

OpenAI's video generation model, known for fine detail and physics simulation. Lighting, materials, and motion trajectories look more realistic than most competitors.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-video with top-tier visual quality
- Strong physics consistency (lighting, reflections, motion)
- Video stitching and continuation support

## Pricing & access
Included with ChatGPT Plus (~20 USD/month) for basic access; Pro plan for higher limits. Available globally.

## Alternatives
Runway Gen-3 (pro editing), Kling (stronger long videos), Pika (faster, lighter).

## Who it's for
Creators wanting high-quality video for ads and pre-vis, willing to pay for top-tier output.
