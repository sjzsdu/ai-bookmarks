DomoAI made its name on live-action-to-anime conversion: drop in footage, pick a style, get a re-drawn animated clip. Its image-to-video keeps motion restrained — better for breathing life into illustrations than big action.

## Strengths
- Video style transfer across many anime looks
- Natural micro-motion img2vid
- Discord bot plus web app
- Upscaling and frame interpolation included

## Pricing & access
Free quota; paid from ~$9.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Jimeng and Kling cover some img2vid cases with direct access; no direct rival for the style-transfer quality.

## Bottom line
A sharp tool for fan edits and stylization — mind the copyright boundaries.
