Tencent made Hunyuan3D the infrastructure of AI 3D: unlimited free generation on the official site, open weights for self-hosting. One image in, a textured 3D model out in under a minute. Quality leads the open field; topology still needs work, but free unlimited iteration changes the game itself.

## Strengths
- Image-to-3D (single/multi-image)
- Auto UV and texturing
- Unlimited free official web app
- Open weights for GPU self-hosting

## Pricing & access
Completely free. Direct access.

## Compared
Tripo (more polished product), Rodin (higher precision); Hunyuan wins on free and open.

## Bottom line
The inclusive entry point and price anchor of AI 3D.
