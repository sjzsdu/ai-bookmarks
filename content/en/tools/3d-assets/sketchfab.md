# Sketchfab

Online platform for showcasing and trading 3D models. The killer feature is the online 3D viewer — inspect models in your browser without downloading anything. Lots of free and paid models, and you can upload your own work to sell.

## Strengths
- Online 3D model viewer
- Large free and paid model library
- AR/VR showcase support
- Upload and sell models

## Pricing & Access
- Many free models
- Downloadizer Pro: $12/month
- Credit card required
- May require VPN from China

## Alternatives
- TurboSquid
- CGTrader
- Free3D

## Who it's for
3D designers, developers looking for ready-made models, portfolio showcase.
