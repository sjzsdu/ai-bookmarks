Sketchfab is the YouTube-plus-store of 3D: millions of models preview in real time from any angle, mixing community portfolios with a paid marketplace.

## Getting started

Filter by license first (downloadable/commercial), then sort by polygon count — game projects want low-poly. Spin the preview to check wireframe and textures before buying.

## What you can actually do

- Store: buyout purchases with clear licensing
- Free zone: CC-licensed models on tap
- Portfolios: the industry-standard 3D viewer

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Every model carries its own license — verify per asset before commercial use. Popular models are widely reused; check for duplicates in brand work. Export formats vary.

## Alternatives

Turbosquid for higher-poly batches, Poly Haven for free CC0, Meshy and Tripo for generation.
