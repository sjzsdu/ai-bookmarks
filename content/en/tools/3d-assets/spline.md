Spline is 'Figma for 3D': model, texture, and animate in the browser, then embed the result in a webpage via iframe or code. It's less a modeling tool than a 'put 3D to work' tool.

## Getting started

Start from official templates and learn the scene-object-material-motion structure; the usual orb-and-text-with-glow combo covers most landing pages. Iframe embed is the fastest export.

## What you can actually do

- Web embeds: interactive scenes hosted on your site
- Motion: keyframes and physics tuned visually
- Sharing: live links for team review

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Complex scenes strain low-end devices — watch polygon and light counts on mobile. Fine modeling is out of scope; import complex meshes from elsewhere.

## Alternatives

Three.js for coded interactive 3D, Unity for engine-grade effects, Sketchfab for pure model hosting.
