Kaedim runs the 'AI plus human QA' route: upload 2D concept art, AI generates the model, and a human modeler fixes it before delivery — real game-engine-ready assets. The premium price buys determinism that saves days of modeler time per asset.

## Strengths
- 2D concepts → production 3D models
- Human QA and correction
- Game-engine-friendly topology
- Batch asset pipelines

## Pricing & access
Studio-tier subscription. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Tripo and Hunyuan3D (pure AI, cheaper, no human backstop).

## Bottom line
Money for time: funded teams buy certainty.
