CSM explored image-to-3D early and now positions as the developer's API: commerce platforms turning product photos into 3D viewers, education tools letting students upload sketches — integrate and go. The web app works by hand, but its real value is pipelines.

## Strengths
- Image/sketch-to-3D API
- Real-time preview generation
- Multi-format export with PBR maps
- Batch pipelines

## Pricing & access
Free tier; usage-based. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Hunyuan3D offers a free-quota API — often the first look for domestic integration.

## Bottom line
Infrastructure supply for the 3D-generation wave.
