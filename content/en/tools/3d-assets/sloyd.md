Sloyd bets on one niche: game props. Instead of photo generation, it uses parametric templates — pick a 'treasure chest', drag sliders for size, ornamentation, and wear, and get a low-poly, clean-topology model ready for Unity or Unreal. For indie devs dressing levels, it beats photo-to-3D in practice.

## Strengths
- Parametric game-prop generation
- Real-time slider editing
- Low-poly optimization and LODs
- Engine-friendly export

## Pricing & access
Free tier; from ~$9.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Hunyuan3D and Tripo (generative); no domestic parametric equivalent.

## Bottom line
Pragmatism inside game-asset generation.
