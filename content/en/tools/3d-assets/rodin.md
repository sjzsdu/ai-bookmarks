Rodin (Hyper3D) goes furthest on the question 'can AI 3D be production-real?': multi-image generation yields high-precision geometry with relatively clean topology, ready for Blender or ZBrush. Game and film teams already use it for asset pre-production. This is the professional ceiling of AI 3D.

## Strengths
- Single/multi-image high-precision mesh
- Auto topology and UVs
- PBR texture generation
- OBJ/FBX/GLB export

## Pricing & access
Free tier; subscription. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Tripo (same Chinese team family, VAST) and Tencent Hunyuan3D (free).

## Bottom line
The head of professional-grade AI 3D generation.
