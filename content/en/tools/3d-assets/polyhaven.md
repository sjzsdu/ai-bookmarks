# Poly Haven

The free paradise of the 3D world. All assets are CC0 licensed — use them commercially however you want. High-quality HDR environment maps, PBR materials, and 3D models. An essential resource for anyone doing 3D rendering.

## Strengths
- CC0 license, completely free for commercial use
- High-quality HDR environment maps
- PBR material textures
- Growing 3D model collection

## Pricing & Access
- Completely free
- Direct download, no registration
- May be slow from China

## Alternatives
- ambientCG (also CC0)
- HDRi Haven (same team)
- FreePBR

## Who it's for
3D rendering artists, game developers, architectural visualization designers.
