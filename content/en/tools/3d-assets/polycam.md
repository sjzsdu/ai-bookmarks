Polycam turns 3D scanning into a phone app: circle an object with a LiDAR iPhone and get an exportable model in minutes; photo mode covers the rest. Room-scale capture adds floor plans and measurements. The lowest-friction entry to real-world 3D assets.

## Strengths
- Object scanning (LiDAR/photo)
- Room-scale capture with measurements
- OBJ/GLB/FBX exports
- Web viewing and sharing

## Pricing & access
Free tier; Pro subscription. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Luma AI (same lane, strong quality); no direct domestic phone-scanning rival.

## Bottom line
The definitive consumer-grade 3D scanner.
