Turbosquid is the veteran of 3D marketplaces — largest and most professional: furniture to film-grade characters, filterable by polygon count, format, and license, with the Checkmate certification making 'is this model usable' quantifiable.

## Getting started

Pin down your engine's format requirements, then filter by Checkmate tags (game-ready/film); check a seller's ratings over their preview renders.

## What you can actually do

- Pro marketplace: film- and CAD-grade assets
- Spec filters: poly count, format, UV status
- Enterprise: team accounts and volume licensing

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Quality costs — film-grade assets often run tens to hundreds of dollars. Read license types carefully; royalty-free and editorial use differ sharply.

## Alternatives

Sketchfab for community and lower prices, Poly Haven for free CC0, Meshy or Rodin for generation.
