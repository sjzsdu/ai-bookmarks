Tripo (from VAST) sells one thing: speed. Upload an image and get a 3D model in seconds. Not always the highest quality, but the fastest way to see a shape in 3D, with a generous free tier.

## Getting started

Pick images with clear silhouettes and clean backgrounds; judge overall proportions first, details later. Regenerating from a different angle beats fiddling with parameters.

## What you can actually do

- Image-to-3D: one photo to a model
- Text-to-3D: prompt straight to mesh
- Online edits: basic trimming and texture tweaks

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Back-side detail is essentially guessed from one image — provide multiple views for important shots. Topology comes messy; decimate and clean before engine import.

## Alternatives

Rodin for precision, Hunyuan3D for free unlimited, Sloyd for engine-friendly prop topology.
