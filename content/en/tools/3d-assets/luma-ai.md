# Luma AI

Turn your phone into a 3D scanner. Shoot video around any object and Luma AI reconstructs a high-quality 3D model using NeRF technology. Perfect for digitizing real-world items without knowing how to model.

## Strengths
- Phone video to 3D model
- NeRF-based reconstruction
- Exports common 3D formats
- 360-degree showcase videos

## Pricing & Access
- Free: basic features
- Paid: $10/month starting price
- iOS and Android apps
- May require VPN from China

## Alternatives
- Polycam
- RealityScan (Epic)
- Scan3D

## Who it's for
E-commerce sellers (product 3D displays), collectors, 3D content creators.
