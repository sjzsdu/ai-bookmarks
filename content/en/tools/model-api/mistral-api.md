Mistral is Europe's AI flagship, selling 'good enough and cheap': flagship models near the international second tier, small models (Ministral) extremely efficient, and Codestral well liked for code completion. For European business, data staying in Europe is the hard requirement it meets.

## Strengths
- Full Mistral Large/Small lineup
- Codestral code-specialized model
- Free experimental tier
- EU/GDPR data compliance

## Pricing & access

Usage-based, below comparable US vendors. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
DeepSeek (cheaper, stronger reasoning), Mistral (EU compliance); both are average at Chinese.

## Bottom line

Excellent within its specific compliance and value niche.
