Volcengine Ark is ByteDance opening its internal model infrastructure: Doubao APIs with aggressive pricing — Doubao Lite has long been among the cheapest tiers in the domestic market. The underlying infrastructure is proven at Douyin scale, and third-party models like DeepSeek are aggregated for one-stop access.

## Strengths
- Full Doubao Pro/Lite/vision API lineup
- Third-party model aggregation
- High-concurrency, low-latency inference
- Fine-tuning and evaluation tooling

## Pricing & access

Usage-based with wide price tiers. Direct access.

## Compared
Bailian (broader models), Ark (cheaper); pick by budget and ecosystem.

## Bottom line

A first pick for price-sensitive, high-volume workloads.
