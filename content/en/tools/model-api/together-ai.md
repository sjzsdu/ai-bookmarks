# Together AI

Inference platform for open-source models — fast, cheap, and developer-friendly.

## Strengths
- All major open-source models (LLaMA 3, Mistral, Mixtral)
- Fast inference, cheaper than OpenAI
- Fine-tuning and custom model deployment
- Embedding services included

## Pricing & access
Pay-as-you-go per token, very affordable for open-source models.

## Alternatives
Groq (even faster), Replicate (easier deployment).

## Who it's for
Developers who want cheap, fast access to open-source models via API.
