Bailian packages the Qwen family with a full model-engineering stack — fine-tuning, RAG, agent orchestration. It's the first-party entry for Qwen APIs, OpenAI-compatible to keep migration cheap, with generous new-user credits. For enterprise apps, its knowledge and orchestration layers replace real self-built work.

## Strengths
- Full Qwen API lineup (Max/Plus/Turbo/open weights)
- Fine-tuning and evaluation
- Built-in RAG and knowledge management
- App orchestration and plugins

## Pricing & access

Usage-based with ample free credits. Direct access.

## Compared
Volcano Ark (ByteDance), SiliconFlow (aggregator); Bailian for Alibaba-ecosystem enterprises.

## Bottom line

The comprehensive option among Chinese model APIs — the workhorse for enterprise projects.
