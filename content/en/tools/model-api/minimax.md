MiniMax exposes the multimodal model capabilities behind Hailuo AI. Developers can access text, speech, music, and video APIs from one platform instead of combining multiple providers.

## Strengths
- Conversational text and long-document processing
- Natural text-to-speech and voice capabilities
- Music and video generation APIs
- Usage management and enterprise services in China

## Pricing & access
Usage is billed by model and volume, with different units for each modality. Current rates are listed on the developer platform.

## Who it's for
Teams building AI companions, audio/video creation tools, customer support, and multimodal content products.
