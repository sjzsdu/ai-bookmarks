# n8n

Open-source workflow automation that connects systems with nodes — and can drop LLMs into the flow.

## Why self-host
Data stays on your machine/server; rich nodes for databases, SaaS, webhooks; combine with Dify/Coze APIs for an "AI + business" loop.

## Capabilities
Visual automation, HTTP/DB nodes, AI nodes, schedule/event triggers.

## Who it's for
Ops, IT and developers embedding AI into internal processes.
