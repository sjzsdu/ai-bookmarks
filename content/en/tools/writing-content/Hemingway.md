# Hemingway Editor

Free English writing tool that uses color highlighting to help you write clearer, simpler text. Flags passive voice, long sentences, and excessive modifiers — makes your English read more naturally.

## Strengths
- Readability scoring
- Color-coded issue highlighting
- Sentence simplification suggestions
- Passive voice detection

## Pricing
Completely free web version, desktop app available (paid). Accessible globally.

## Alternatives
No direct equivalent. For Chinese, try MiTie Writing Cat.

## Who it's for
Bloggers, academic writers, anyone wanting to improve English writing clarity.
