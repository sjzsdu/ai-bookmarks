Hemingway Editor does one thing: simpler sentences. Long sentences in yellow, adverbs in blue, passive voice in purple, readability by grade level — a health check for prose that never writes for you.

## Getting started

Paste your draft and edit by color; Grade 8–10 is the comfort zone for most business writing.

## What you can do with it

- Paste a bloated product page and cut every yellow-flagged sentence.
- Check readability pre-publish and move Grade 14 paragraphs to Grade 9.
- Use adverb highlights to quit leaning on 'very' and 'really'.

## Limits and gotchas

English only. The metrics are mechanical — literary writing needn't obey. AI rewrites bill per use.

## Alternatives

Grammarly for grammar plus readability, ProWritingAid for deep reports, human editing for Chinese.
