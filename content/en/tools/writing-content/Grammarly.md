# Grammarly

Essential AI tool for English writing. Grammar, spelling, and punctuation checking are just the basics — Premium suggests sentence optimization, tone adjustment, and repetition elimination. Available as browser plugin, desktop app, and mobile keyboard.

## Strengths
- High-accuracy grammar and spelling check
- Style suggestions: sentence structure, tone, conciseness
- AI rewriting: select and rewrite paragraphs
- Multi-platform: browser, desktop, mobile

## Pricing
Free tier with basic checks, Premium at $12/month for all features. Accessible globally.

## Alternatives
1Checker (Chinese English proofreading tool), Microsoft Word built-in proofing.

## Who it's for
Anyone writing in English — emails, papers, documents. A lifesaver for non-native English speakers.
