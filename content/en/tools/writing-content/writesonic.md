Writesonic is built for the content marketing pipeline: pick a template, feed keywords, produce at volume. Its SEO article generator is the headline act — live web research, keyword analysis, internal-link suggestions, all aimed at search rankings. Ad templates span Google/Facebook to social posts.

## Strengths
- SEO-optimized long-form with web grounding
- 100+ marketing copy templates
- Chatsonic chat + image generation
- Batch generation and API access

## Pricing & access
Free trial; from ~$39/month, credit-based. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
Jasper (pricier, more enterprise), Copy.ai (cheaper, template-first); Writesonic balances SEO best.

## Bottom line
A production tool for content teams — it chose workflow over literary quality.
