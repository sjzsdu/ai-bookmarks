Obsidian Copilot is a community plugin wiring LLMs into your local vault: chat answers grounded in your notes with source links, and local Ollama support keeps data entirely on your machine.

## Getting started

Install from the community plugins, configure an API key or Ollama URL, and set the default vault-grounded mode.

## What you can do with it

- Ask your vault 'what was that deployment plan I noted' and get source-linked answers.
- Wire a local Ollama model so note Q&A never leaves the machine.
- Continue the current note's paragraph in your own writing style.

## Limits and gotchas

Community-maintained quality varies. Model costs are yours. Retrieval on large vaults depends on indexing — tune it.

## Alternatives

Notion AI for cloud-all-in-one, Zotero plugins for literature, dedicated tools for pure writing.
