# Obsidian Copilot

Open-source AI plugin for Obsidian that lets you chat with AI in your knowledge base, reference current notes, search related docs, and generate content. Works well with Obsidian's linking and knowledge graph system.

## Strengths
- AI chat directly in Obsidian
- Note context awareness
- Natural language search across knowledge base
- Content generation within notes

## Pricing
Plugin is free, bring your own API key with pay-per-use costs. Install from Obsidian community plugins.

## Alternatives
Notion AI (paid but simpler), Feishu AI (ByteDance).

## Who it's for
Heavy Obsidian users, knowledge management enthusiasts, anyone wanting AI in notes without switching tools.
