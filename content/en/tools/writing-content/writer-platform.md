Writer sells an enterprise content hub: its own Palmyra models, company terminology, brand voice rules — all outbound copy flows through it, consistent and controlled. Regulated industries like finance and healthcare buy in for the data-safety commitments and auditability.

## Strengths
- Palmyra models with transparent training data
- Enforced terminology, style guides, brand voice
- Team collaboration, approvals, content management
- API and office-suite integrations

## Pricing & access
From ~$18/user/month. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
Jasper wins on marketing templates; Writer wins on governance.

## Bottom line
Skip it as an individual; evaluate seriously as an enterprise content team.
