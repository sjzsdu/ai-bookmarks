Sudowrite is unique among AI writing tools: it serves only fiction. The Story Bible stores characters, lore, and outline as structured context so generation stays aligned — no more eye-color changes in chapter 20. Expansion and rewrite quality genuinely hold up; suggested continuations are often usable as-is.

## Strengths
- Story Bible: characters, world, outline consistency
- Expand, rewrite, shrink, brainstorm
- Style matching across voices
- Chapter-level project management

## Pricing & access
From ~$10/month, credit-based. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
For Chinese web fiction: Caiyun Xiaomeng and Biling AI — weaker but domestic-first.

## Bottom line
Worth paying for serious long-form work; Chinese creators should trial first.
