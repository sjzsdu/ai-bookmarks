# QuillBot

QuillBot specializes in paraphrasing and academic writing support. It can adjust fluency, formality, and length while bringing grammar checking, summarization, and citation tools into one workflow.

## Strengths
- Multiple modes for sentence and paragraph rewriting
- Grammar, spelling, and fluency checks
- Long-text summarization and citation assistance
- Browser and writing-app integrations

## Pricing & access
The free tier provides basic paraphrasing with length limits. Premium adds more modes and higher allowances.

## Who it's for
Students, researchers, language learners, editors, and marketers polishing English-language content.
