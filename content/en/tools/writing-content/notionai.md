# Notion AI

AI built into Notion — use it directly in notes, docs, and knowledge bases for writing, summarizing, translating, and organizing. Integrates well with Notion's database and template system.

## Strengths
- Generate and rewrite content inline
- Auto-summarize long documents
- Multi-language translation
- Extract and organize from databases

## Pricing
Free tier with limited AI uses, Plus plan adds $10/month for more. Works globally, subscription requires international payment.

## Alternatives
Feishu AI (ByteDance, direct access in China), Yuque AI (Alibaba).

## Who it's for
Existing Notion users, knowledge management enthusiasts, anyone wanting AI in their notes without switching tools.
