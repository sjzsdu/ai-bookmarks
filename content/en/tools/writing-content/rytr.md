Rytr's philosophy is small and cheap: 40+ templates covering emails, social posts, and product descriptions, 10k free characters monthly, paid plans from $9. It skips the big-platform bells and whistles but covers everything short-form copy needs — for solopreneurs, value-per-dollar is the whole game.

## Strengths
- 40+ short-copy templates
- Multiple tone options
- Browser extension in any input field
- Built-in plagiarism and grammar checks

## Pricing & access
Free 10k characters/month; ~$9/month paid. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
Copy.ai has more templates at a higher price; Rytr wins on cheap and simple.

## Bottom line
Economy class among AI copy tools — enough, affordable, hassle-free.
