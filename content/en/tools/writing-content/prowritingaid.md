Book authors use it chapter by chapter, like hiring a writing coach. A lifetime license also exists, which wins the long-run math.

## Strengths
- 20+ writing-analysis dimensions
- Real-time corrections plus rewrite suggestions
- Word, browser, and desktop coverage
- Generative AI rewrites (recently added)

## Pricing & access

Grammarly (smoothest UX), Hemingway (free and minimal); ProWritingAid leads on depth.

Treat it as a coach, not a spellchecker — that's where the value is.
