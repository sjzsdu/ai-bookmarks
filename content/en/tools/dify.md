# Dify

An open-source LLM app-development platform combining RAG, Agents, workflows and model routing in a visual UI.

## Why teams like it
Open-source and self-hostable (data autonomy); active Chinese docs/community; easy connection to domestic models (Tongyi, Zhipu, DeepSeek); one platform from prototype to production.

## Capabilities
Prompt orchestration, RAG knowledge base, Agents, visual workflows, API access.

## Who it's for
Companies and developers building their own AI apps/knowledge bases.
