# Magician for Figma

Figma plugin that uses AI to generate icons, images, and copy directly in your design files. No context switching — call AI right in your design workflow.

## Strengths
- AI-generated icons and images
- Text-to-design elements
- Design suggestions and inspiration
- Works directly in Figma

## Pricing
Basic features free, premium at $8/month. Requires free Figma install.

## Alternatives
InstantDesign AI plugins, Canva AI.

## Who it's for
Figma users, UI designers, anyone needing quick design assets in their mockups.
