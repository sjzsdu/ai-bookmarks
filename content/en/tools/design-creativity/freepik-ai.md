Freepik built its name on stock assets and now bundles AI generation: text-to-image across aggregated models (Flux, in-house Mystic), image-to-video, AI retouching, background removal — all inside one subscription. For designers mixing stock and generative work, one subscription covers most of the workflow.

## Strengths
- Multi-model image generation including Mystic
- AI video and image editing
- Millions of assets usable alongside AI output
- Unified commercial license

## Pricing & access
From ~€12/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Chinese stock sites are catching up on AI but trail on scale and integration.

## Bottom line
The model case for stock-site AI pivots: not the deepest tool, but having everything counts.
