Motiff is a homegrown Figma counterpart: editor capability (vectors, auto-layout, component systems, collaboration) at Figma level, while answering the two pain points of Chinese teams — stable access and price. AI is the differentiator: screen drafts, smart layout, and an AI magic-wand for batch property edits.

## Strengths
- Figma-grade vector editing and auto-layout
- Component systems with real-time collaboration
- AI UI generation, smart layout, magic wand
- Figma file import

## Pricing & access
Free editing; per-seat collaboration below Figma pricing. Direct access.

## Compared
Pixso and MasterGo are similar domestic options; Motiff leads on AI and editor completeness.

## Bottom line
The pragmatic pick for Chinese UI teams: parity features, lower cost, no network anxiety.
