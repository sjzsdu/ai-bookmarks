Jimeng is ByteDance's creator platform, with Seedream ranking first-tier in China for text-to-image, plus image-to-video and template layout — a full path from idea to deliverable.

## Getting started

Describe the scene in Chinese, controlling subject, style, and lighting; use built-in layout for poster text instead of exporting elsewhere.

## What you can actually do

- Text-to-image for commercial visuals
- Image-to-video for animated shots
- Smart layout for poster and cover typography

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Copyright and commercial terms shift with platform policy — confirm on big projects. Popular styles converge; differentiation lives in your prompting.

## Alternatives

LiblibAI for open models, Midjourney overseas, Meitu Design Studio for product photos.
