# Jimeng Design

Jimeng Design applies Seedream image models to commercial creative workflows. Designers can start from Chinese prompts or references, explore visual directions, and refine usable assets through editing and style controls.

## Strengths
- Visuals for posters, ecommerce, and social media
- Reference images, inpainting, and outpainting
- Chinese prompts and varied commercial styles
- A connected path into Jimeng's video generation

## Pricing & access
Free trial credits are available. Additional generations and advanced capabilities may require a membership or credits.

## Who it's for
Brand, ecommerce, advertising, and social media teams, plus designers producing creative concepts quickly.
