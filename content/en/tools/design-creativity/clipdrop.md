# Clipdrop

AI image toolkit — one stop for common image tasks: background removal, cleanup, upscaling, generation, text removal. Each feature is focused and reliable.

## Strengths
- Background removal
- Cleanup: remove objects from images
- Image upscaling: low-res to high-res
- AI generation: text to image
- Text Remover: remove text from images

## Pricing
Free trial for each feature, API subscription at $9/month. Accessible globally.

## Alternatives
Gaoding Design, Meitu Xiuxiu, Pixian.ai.

## Who it's for
Designers, e-commerce sellers, anyone needing quick image processing without Photoshop.
