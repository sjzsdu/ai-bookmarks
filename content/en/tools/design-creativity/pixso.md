Pixso bets on integration: whiteboard, prototype, UI design, and handoff specs in one tool, so small teams stop switching between four apps. The editor covers Figma's core feature set, servers sit in China, and the free tier is friendly to individuals.

## Strengths
- UI + prototype + whiteboard + handoff specs
- Component variants and auto-layout
- Real-time multi-user collaboration
- Figma/Sketch import

## Pricing & access
Free for individuals; per-seat for teams. Direct access.

## Compared
MasterGo and Motiff are similar domestic alternatives; Pixso wins on flow coverage.

## Bottom line
The whole design toolchain in one domestic app — good for trimming tool budgets.
