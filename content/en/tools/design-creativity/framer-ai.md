Framer evolved from design tool to site builder, and AI generation is its calling card: one prompt yields a full landing page with typography and polish visibly above template-builder AI. The output is a real editable canvas — every element changeable — and publishing with hosting is one click. Motion capability tops the category.

## Strengths
- AI text-to-full-page generation
- Freeform visual canvas editing
- Built-in hosting, custom domains, SEO
- Advanced motion and interactive components

## Pricing & access
Free with a Framer subdomain; from ~$10/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
No direct domestic rival; China hosting also needs ICP filing.

## Bottom line
Best value for presentation sites: design quality, speed, and cost in balance.
