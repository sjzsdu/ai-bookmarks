Gaoding is the Canva of the Chinese web: campaign posters, WeChat covers, product images, decks — every Chinese graphic scenario has a template library behind it. AI features focus on practical retouching — cutout, object removal, expansion — not flashy but frequently used. For operations staff it's a daily production tool.

## Strengths
- Huge Chinese template library
- AI cutout, erase, expand, enhance
- Batch design driven by data
- Enterprise brand template management

## Pricing & access

Rich free tier; membership ~¥179/year. Direct access.

## Compared
Chuangkiti (similar), Canva (international); Gaoding is smoothest for Chinese operations work.

## Bottom line
A consumables library for Chinese graphic design: fast, complete, reliable.
