# Figma AI

AI features built into Figma — auto-suggest layouts, generate component variants, rename layers intelligently. A UI designer's efficiency tool that stays inside Figma.

## Strengths
- Auto layout suggestions
- Component variant generation
- Smart layer renaming
- Design-to-code conversion

## Pricing
Figma free tier has basic AI features, Professional at $15/month. Accessible globally, subscription requires international payment.

## Alternatives
InstantDesign AI (Chinese UI tool, direct access), MasterGo AI.

## Who it's for
UI/UX designers, product managers, anyone needing quick design mockups.
