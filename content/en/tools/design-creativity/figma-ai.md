Figma's AI plays native: not a side product but features like First Draft, layer renames, and copy replacement embedded in the editor you already use — an accelerator, not a novelty.

## Getting started

Generate an initial layout with First Draft and keep editing the closest version; pair with auto-renames and batch copy replacement to keep files clean.

## What you can actually do

- First Draft: prompt to editable screens
- File hygiene: AI layer naming and organization
- Content replace: placeholders to real copy

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Draft quality leans on your prompt and existing design system. AI features need paid seats; availability varies by region.

## Alternatives

Motiff and Pixso domestically, Framer for sites, v0 for pure AI UI generation.
