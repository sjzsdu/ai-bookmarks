# GitHub Copilot

Microsoft's AI code-completion tool, built on OpenAI models and integrated into mainstream IDEs — the most adopted "AI pair programmer."

## Strengths
Inline completion, whole-function generation, Copilot Chat, terminal suggestions.

## Pricing
Around 10 USD/month for individuals, 19 USD/seat for business.

## Alternatives
Cursor for AI-native editing, Codeium for free tier, and domestic options like Tongyi Lingma for Chinese teams.

## Who it's for
Engineers writing code daily; free only for students and some OSS contributors.
