Tianyin is the easiest AI songwriting entry for Chinese content: enter lyrics or a topic, pick a style, and get a full song with vocals in minutes. For non-arrangers it cuts the cost of 'writing a song' to nearly zero; for musicians, melody and MIDI exports serve as raw material.

## Strengths
- One-click complete songs (melody, arrangement, vocals)
- Custom lyrics with natural Chinese phrasing
- Style templates: pop, guofeng, electronic, and more
- Audio and MIDI export for further editing

## Pricing & access
Generous free tier; membership for commercial use. Direct access.

## Alternatives
Suno and Udio lead on overall polish and English songs but need a proxy.

## Bottom line
The go-to free option for fast Chinese songs; serious production still belongs to ACE Studio or human workflows.
