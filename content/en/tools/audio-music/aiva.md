AIVA was early to focus specifically on scoring: film, games, and ads need mood-matched background music, not full pop songs — and that's all AIVA does. Its unique edge is MIDI and sheet-music export, so the generated track is a starting point musicians can rewrite.

## Strengths
- Generation by mood, genre, and duration
- MIDI/score export into any DAW
- Style models spanning cinematic, electronic, classical
- Full commercial rights on paid plans

## Pricing & access
Free for non-commercial; standard from ~€11/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Suno (full songs with vocals), NetEase Tianyin (domestic scoring tool).

## Bottom line
BGM productivity, not a song generator — a dependable pick for video and game teams.
