Suno turned songwriting into ordering: describe a style, add lyrics (or let it write them), and two minutes later a complete song arrives — vocals, arrangement, harmonies. Its English output leads the field.

## Getting started

Be specific: genre, mood, instruments, theme. Paste lyrics with [Verse]/[Chorus] markers. Generate 3–4 versions and iterate on the strongest.

## What you can actually do

- Full songs: vocals, arrangement, harmonies in one pass
- Custom lyrics with structure and style tags
- Extend: grow a clip into a full track

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Copyright ownership varies by subscription tier — read terms before commercial use. Quality sags past ~3 minutes; generate in sections. Chinese enunciation can blur.

## Alternatives

Udio for audio purists, Stable Audio for instrumentals, NetEase Tianyin as a free domestic pick, ACE Studio for produced vocals.
