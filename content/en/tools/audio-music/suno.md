# Suno

Generate a full song (melody + vocals + arrangement) from a sentence — one of the two dominant AI music tools alongside Udio.

## Strengths
- Text-to-song from a single sentence
- Wide genre selection (pop, rock, electronic, classical)
- Vocals and arrangement generated together; clips extendable

## Pricing & access
Free tier (~5 songs/day); paid plans from 8 USD/month. Generally accessible directly.

## Alternatives
Udio (better arrangement detail), AIVA (classical), Soundraw (loop-based).

## Who it's for
Short-video scoring, independent musicians, demo creators. Final release benefits from human mixing.
