# Udio

One of the two dominant AI music generators (the other being Suno), with slightly better arrangement detail and audio quality.

## Strengths
- Text-to-song with multiple genres (pop, rock, electronic, classical)
- Top-tier arrangement detail and audio quality among AI music tools
- Supports lyrics input and style specification

## Pricing & access
Free tier available; paid plans from 10 USD/month. Generally accessible directly.

## Alternatives
Suno (competitor), AIVA (classical/orchestral), Soundraw (loop-based).

## Who it's for
Independent musicians, demo creators, short-video scoring.
