From Stability AI, Stable Audio's differentiator is short audio: SFX, samples, loops, and duration-locked BGM. Game and short-video creators often need a three-second ability sound, not a song — that's exactly its home turf.

## Strengths
- Text-to-SFX, samples, and music
- Precise output duration control
- Clean commercial licensing via AudioSparx training data
- Audio style transfer and upscaling

## Pricing & access
Monthly free quota; paid from ~$11.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
No direct domestic SFX rival; for general scoring try NetEase Tianyin.

## Bottom line
An SFX production line for games and video — don't ask it to sing.
