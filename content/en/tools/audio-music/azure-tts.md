# Microsoft Azure TTS

Microsoft's speech synthesis service: multilingual neural voices, enterprise-stable, customizable. Available in China via 21Vianet for compliance.

## Strengths
- Multilingual TTS, emotional speech
- Real-time/batch synthesis, fine SSML control
- Custom voice (application required)
- SLA保障 with high concurrency

## Pricing & access
Pay-as-you-go with free tier. Azure China (21Vianet) enables compliant in-region calls with RMB billing.

## Alternatives
Amazon Polly (AWS), Google Cloud TTS, ElevenLabs (higher naturalness).

## Who it's for
Apps, call centers, navigation, audiobook platforms — any production environment needing stable voice output.
