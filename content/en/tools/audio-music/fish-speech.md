# Fish Audio

TTS and voice cloning platform with natural voice quality. Strong Chinese support among similar tools. Clone a voice with just a few seconds of sample audio.

## Strengths
- High-quality TTS with natural voice output
- Voice cloning from short audio samples
- Multi-language support (Chinese, English, etc.)
- Easy API integration

## Pricing & access
Free tier available, pay-as-you-go. Direct access in most regions.
