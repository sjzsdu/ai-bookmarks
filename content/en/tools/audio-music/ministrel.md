MiniStrel is MiniMax's music generation model, and its edge is Chinese songwriting: clear articulation, melodies that respect the tones of Chinese lyrics, and fast full-song output for demos.

## Getting started

Specify style, mood, and lyric structure (verse/chorus) up front; generate 2–3 versions and pick. Rewrite lyrics before spamming reruns.

## What you can actually do

- Song generation: lyrics + style to full track
- Instrumental mode: no-vocal backing tracks
- Style control: pop, folk, electronic basics

## Trade-offs and gotchas

Long lyrics get truncated — generate in sections and stitch. Commercial terms follow MiniMax's platform policy; confirm before release. English songs trail Chinese.

## Alternatives

Suno and Udio for English and polish, NetEase Tianyin as a free domestic option, ACE Studio for produced vocals.
