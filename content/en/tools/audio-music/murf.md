Murf plays the enterprise route: voices are stable and polished rather than dramatically emotive — right for training videos, product explainers, and IVR. The sentence-based editor lets you tune pitch, speed, and pauses line by line, like writing a score for your voiceover.

## Strengths
- 120+ languages/accents with realistic voices
- Sentence-level pitch, speed, and pause control
- Voice cloning on paid tiers
- Team collaboration and enterprise compliance

## Pricing & access
10-minute free trial; paid from ~$19/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
ElevenLabs for expressiveness and cloning; MoYu Gongfang (魔音工坊) for Chinese-first workflows.

## Bottom line
A batch, multilingual, collaborative voiceover pipeline — individual hobbyists may find better value elsewhere.
