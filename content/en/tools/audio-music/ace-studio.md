ACE Studio solves one specific problem: singing. Give it melody and lyrics and it performs with near-human AI vocals — breaths, vibrato, and articulation all adjustable. Unlike all-in-one song generators such as Suno, it lives inside the production chain as a DAW plugin, outputting vocal tracks ready for mixing.

## Strengths
- MIDI + lyrics → professional AI vocals
- Rich multilingual voice banks
- Parametric control of breaths, vibrato, ornaments
- VST/AU plugin integration

## Pricing & access
Free trial; from ~¥68/month, direct access and local payment.

## Alternatives
Synthesizer V (finer control), X Studio (free-er, from iFlytek).

## Bottom line
A musician's tool, not a toy: melody in, mix-ready vocals out.
