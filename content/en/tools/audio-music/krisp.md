Krisp generates nothing — it just makes your microphone sound clean. An AI model separates voice from noise in real time (keyboards, fans, traffic, dogs) and exposes itself as a virtual mic for Zoom, Teams, OBS, and anything else, with zero workflow changes.

## Strengths
- Real-time noise, echo, and volume adaptive processing
- Virtual device plugs into any call or recording app
- Meeting transcription and AI notes
- On-device processing — audio never leaves your machine

## Pricing & access
60 free minutes daily; ~$8/month. Proxy may be needed for download in some regions.

## Alternatives
Tencent Meeting and Feishu have built-in noise suppression; NVIDIA Broadcast for NVIDIA GPU owners.

## Bottom line
Small tool, big value: $8/month for a clean voice is easy math for heavy callers.
