# Pika

An AI video tool that went viral for being fun, fast and easy — turning ideas into short clips quickly.

Text/image-to-video, video stylization, local animation, sound effects.

## Who it's for

## Note
Length and consistency are limited; longer narratives need Runway or Kling.
