Trae lowers the barrier for trying an AI IDE in China: start with a non-critical project and one small feature. Its free tier and model policy can change, so do not build a critical workflow around it alone.

ByteDance's AI IDE, built on VS Code with completely free AI features. Well-optimized for Chinese developers with good support for Chinese comments, requirements, and documentation.

## Strengths
- Free unlimited AI code completion and generation
- Built-in AI Chat with context references
- Builder mode: describe requirements, auto-generate projects
- Supports Claude, GPT, and other major models

## Pricing
Completely free during promotion period. No payment required.

## Alternatives
Tongyi Lingma (Alibaba, completion-focused), CodeGeeX (Zhipu AI).

## Who it's for
Developers wanting a free AI IDE, students, budget-conscious developers. Great opportunity to try during the promotion.
