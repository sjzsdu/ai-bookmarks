Lovable is great for turning a real user flow into a clickable prototype—describe entry, action, and outcome, not “make a SaaS.” Hand off auth, payments, and complex data early when the prototype becomes serious.

Lovable became a breakout conversational full-stack web app builder in 2025. Describe a product and it generates the interface, application logic, and common data integrations, then lets you refine everything through chat.

## Strengths
- Generate working web apps from natural-language requirements
- Build interfaces and common backend integrations together
- Iterate on design and functionality with a live preview
- Connect to GitHub for code ownership and continued development

## Pricing & access
A limited free tier is available, with paid plans starting around $20/month. Usage allowances and team features vary by plan.

## Who it's for
Indie developers, product managers, designers, and startup teams that need to validate an MVP quickly.
