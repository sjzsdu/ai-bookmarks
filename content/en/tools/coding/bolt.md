# Bolt

StackBlitz's AI full-stack builder. Describe your app in the browser, get a runnable full-stack application. Built on WebContainer technology, no local setup needed for preview and editing.

## Strengths
- Generate complete full-stack apps from descriptions
- Live preview and editing in browser
- Supports React, Vue, Next.js and more
- One-click deploy to Netlify

## Pricing
Free tier with daily generation limits, Pro at $20/month. Accessible directly in browser worldwide.

## Alternatives
Replit (similar concept, more general), LOCALLY (Chinese low-code platform).

## Who it's for
Entrepreneurs validating ideas, full-stack developers, non-frontend specialists needing quick pages.
