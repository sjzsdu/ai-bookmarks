LiblibAI is essentially China's Civitai: the largest domestic library of SD, FLUX, and style LoRAs — and you can generate right on the site instead of downloading models. It drops the barrier to playing with open models down to signing up.

## Strengths
- Massive model/LoRA library with Chinese-language browsing
- Online text-to-image and image-to-image, plus cloud ComfyUI workflows
- Train your own LoRA by uploading a dataset
- One-click reuse of community prompt parameters

## Pricing & access
Free credits on signup and daily check-ins; pay-per-credit after that. Direct access from China, local payment methods supported.

## Alternatives
Civitai (bigger Western community, proxy required), Tongyi Wanxiang (simpler, zero-parameter experience).

## Bottom line
The default choice for open-model fans who don't want to manage hardware; be careful uploading commercially sensitive assets.
