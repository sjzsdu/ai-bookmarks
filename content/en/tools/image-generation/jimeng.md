Jimeng Image is ByteDance's AI image creation service. Seedream models turn Chinese descriptions, reference images, and editing instructions into visual assets that can also feed into Jimeng's video workflow.

## Strengths
- Text-to-image and reference-based generation
- Inpainting, outpainting, and style adjustment
- Strong understanding of Chinese and detailed prompts
- Integrated path from still images to AI video

## Pricing & access
Free trial credits are available. Additional generations and advanced features may require a membership or credits.

## Who it's for
Content creators, ecommerce designers, social media teams, and anyone producing concept art quickly.
