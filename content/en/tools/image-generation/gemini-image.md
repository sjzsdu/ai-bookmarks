Nano Banana is the popular nickname for Gemini's image generation and editing capabilities. It places image creation inside a conversation, supporting iterative edits, reference composition, and consistent subjects.

## Strengths
- Text-to-image and reference-based generation
- Multi-turn conversational editing
- Multi-image composition and subject consistency
- Developer experimentation through Google AI Studio

## Pricing & access
Google products typically include limited free use, while API access is billed by model and usage. Availability and allowances vary by region.

## Who it's for
Creators and developers iterating on visual concepts, character assets, or image-enabled applications.
