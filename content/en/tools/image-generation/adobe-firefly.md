Firefly is the most corporate-safe of the major generators: trained on Adobe Stock and licensed content, with indemnification for commercial use. Its raw quality is not the industry ceiling, but its real value is living inside Photoshop — Generative Fill, expand, and background swaps without leaving your workflow.

## Strengths
- Text-to-image, Generative Fill, generative expand
- Structure and style references outperform most closed tools on composition control
- Deep integration with Photoshop, Illustrator, and Express
- Content Credentials label AI involvement by default

## Pricing & access
Free tier is watermarked; full access via Creative Cloud. Web access may require a proxy in some regions.

## Alternatives
Midjourney (higher ceiling, no indemnification), Stable Diffusion (local and free, more setup), Ideogram (better text rendering).

## Bottom line
Not the flashiest generator, but the combination of commercial safety and zero workflow friction makes it the default for enterprise design teams.
