# Kling Image

Image generation from Kuaishou's Kling platform, sharing an account system with Kling video. Accurate Chinese prompt understanding, handles Chinese-style, realistic, and anime aesthetics.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-image with multiple styles
- Strong Chinese prompt comprehension — no need to write English
- Integrated with Kling video for image-to-video workflows

## Pricing & access
Direct access in China, RMB pay-as-you-go with free tier. No VPN or foreign payment needed.

## Alternatives
Tongyi Wanxiang (Alibaba), ERNIE Bot (Baidu), Midjourney (global, needs VPN).

## Who it's for
China-based creators, e-commerce teams, anyone needing quick images to pair with video production.
