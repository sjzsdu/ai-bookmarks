Krea's differentiator is realtime: sketch a few strokes and the AI renders alongside you, changing with every edit. That feedback loop turns composition and color exploration from slot-pulling into a conversation. Beyond images it aggregates video generation, upscalers, and model training — closer to a creative workbench.

## Strengths
- Realtime canvas with millisecond feedback
- Multi-model aggregation including the Flux family
- Image enhancer scaling up to 22K
- Personal style model training

## Pricing & access
Daily free quota; paid from ~$24/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Jimeng and Tongyi Wanxiang (strong generators without realtime canvas).

## Bottom line
Treat it as a visual thinking tool rather than a render farm — that's where it shines.
