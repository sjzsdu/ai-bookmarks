# Tongyi Wanxiang

Alibaba Tongyi's AI painting tool with strong Chinese prompt understanding, integrated into the Tongyi ecosystem. Direct access in China, RMB billing — no VPN hassle.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-image, style transfer, AI posters/e-commerce visuals
- Accurate Chinese semantic understanding
- Clear commercial licensing for domestic use

## Pricing & access
Direct access in China, free tier available, RMB pay-as-you-go. Alibaba Cloud account required.

## Alternatives
Kling Image (Kuaishou), ERNIE Bot (Baidu), Midjourney (global, needs VPN).

## Who it's for
E-commerce ops, self-media creators, China design teams wanting instant, compliant image production.
