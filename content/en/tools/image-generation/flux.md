# Flux

Open-source text-to-image from Black Forest Labs (FLUX.1 family) — among the best quality you can get from open weights, with excellent prompt following.

## Strengths
- Rich detail in text-to-image; stronger text rendering than most open models
- Multiple variants (dev / schnell / pro) trading speed for quality
- Good ComfyUI / WebUI ecosystem; LoRA fine-tuning supported

## Pricing & access
Fully open-source and free. Cloud APIs available on Replicate, fal.ai, etc. for pay-as-you-go.

## Alternatives
Stable Diffusion (mature ecosystem), Midjourney (higher out-of-box quality), Tongyi Wanxiang (online, no GPU needed).

## Who it's for
Designers and developers with GPU resources who want top-tier open-source image quality.
