# Ideogram

Tackles the "garbled text" problem in AI images — text in generated pictures is accurate and readable, far better than most competitors.

## Strengths
- Accurate text rendering for both English and Chinese
- Multiple style switches (realistic, illustration, 3D, anime)
- Magic Prompt auto-optimizes your prompts

## Pricing & access
Limited free tier; Plus plan ~20 USD/month. Generally accessible directly in most regions.

## Alternatives
Midjourney (more stylization), DALL-E 3 (good text via ChatGPT), Canva AI (integrated design tool).

## Who it's for
Designers, marketers, and content creators who need images with readable text.
