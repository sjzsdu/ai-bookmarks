# Leonardo.ai

Full-featured AI art platform with model training (LoRA fine-tuning), a model marketplace, and asset management — built for teams needing consistent, batch-generated visuals.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-image, AI Canvas editing
- Model marketplace with community fine-tunes
- Batch generation and asset management

## Pricing & access
Free daily credits; paid plans from 12 USD/month. Generally accessible directly in most regions.

## Alternatives
Midjourney (higher quality, less control), Stable Diffusion (more DIY), Adobe Firefly (integrated with Creative Cloud).

## Who it's for
Game art, e-commerce design, teams needing style-consistent batch output.
