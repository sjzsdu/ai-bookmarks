Civitai is unavoidable in open-source AI art: nearly every popular checkpoint and LoRA debuts here. Beyond downloads, each community image carries its full model, parameters, and prompt — a giant reproducible gallery. If you run ComfyUI or SD WebUI, you will end up here.

## Strengths
- Enormous library of checkpoints, LoRAs, and embeddings
- Reproducible parameters on every showcase image
- Version histories, reviews, and VRAM guidance
- Paid on-site generation

## Pricing & access
Free downloads; generation via Buzz credits. Proxy required in China.

## Caveat
The community hosts uncensored content — mind compliance in corporate settings and check each model's license.
