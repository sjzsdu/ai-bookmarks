# Midjourney

Known for exceptional image quality, Midjourney excels at artistic and conceptual visuals. Mature prompt community, fast iteration, accessible via Discord or web app.

## Strengths
- Text-to-image, image-to-image, stylization, high-res upscaling
- Huge prompt community for reference and inspiration
- Fast generation with top-tier quality among closed-source tools

## Pricing & access
From ~10 USD/month; no free tier. Available globally via web app.

## Alternatives
DALL-E 3 (via ChatGPT), Stable Diffusion (open source, local), Leonardo.ai (more customization).

## Who it's for
Illustrators, designers, marketing teams, concept artists who want high-quality output without setup.
