SeaArt wins on volume: generous free credits, a community library strong in anime and stylized art, and fast generation. The interface is far friendlier than ComfyUI — pick a model, type a prompt, get images.

## Strengths
- Text-to-image, img2img, inpainting, upscaling
- Community models with an especially rich anime selection
- Mobile app synced with web progress
- Personal LoRA training supported

## Pricing & access
Daily free credits; ~¥30/month unlocks faster queues. Direct access from China.

## Alternatives
LiblibAI (more models, more pro-oriented), Jimeng (stronger video integration).

## Caveat
Review the licensing terms for free-tier outputs before using them in serious commercial work.
