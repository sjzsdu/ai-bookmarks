# Stable Diffusion

Fully open-source text-to-image family (SD1.5, SDXL, community fine-tunes). Biggest advantage: local deployment, no data leaves your machine, no subscription fees.

## Strengths
- Text/image-to-image, inpainting, LoRA style training
- Massive plugin ecosystem (ControlNet, IP-Adapter, etc.)
- Open weights, offline operation, full data control

## Pricing & access
Free and open-source; hardware cost only. Weights downloadable from HuggingFace mirrors.

## Alternatives
Midjourney (higher quality, subscription), DALL-E 3 (via ChatGPT), Leonardo.ai (easier workflow).

## Who it's for
Privacy-focused teams, batch asset production, custom-style training — users willing to invest setup time for full control.
