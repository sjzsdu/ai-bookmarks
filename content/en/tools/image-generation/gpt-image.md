GPT Image is OpenAI's flagship image generation and editing capability. It follows detailed instructions, renders text inside images, and supports iterative changes through natural-language feedback.

## Strengths
- Text-to-image and reference-image editing
- Multi-turn natural-language revisions
- Text rendering for posters, signs, and graphics
- Available through ChatGPT and APIs

## Pricing & access
ChatGPT plans include varying image allowances, while API usage is billed by generation settings and volume. Regional availability follows OpenAI policies.

## Who it's for
Marketing teams, designers, content creators, and developers integrating image generation into products.
