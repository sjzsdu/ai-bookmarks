Rows reimagines the spreadsheet: cells can call APIs (stocks, weather, social metrics) or invoke AI ('summarize this column's trend'). Share a sheet and the recipient sees an auto-refreshing dashboard. A new category between Excel and BI, delightful for lightweight workflows.

## Strengths
- 50+ API integrations as functions
- AI analysis functions (summarize, classify, extract)
- One-click conversion to live dashboards
- Scheduled auto-refresh

## Pricing & access
Free tier; from ~$15/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
No direct domestic equivalent; lightweight BI via Huoban or vika.

## Bottom line
'A spreadsheet that knows the internet' — the sweet spot for light data work.
