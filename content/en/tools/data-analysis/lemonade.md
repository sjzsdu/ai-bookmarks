# Lemonade

Explore data like you're chatting with someone. Connect your data source, ask questions in natural language, and Lemonade queries the data and shows visual results. Way easier than writing SQL for quick data exploration.

## Strengths
- Conversational data querying
- Auto-generates charts and dashboards
- Multiple data source support
- Share analysis results

## Pricing & Access
- Free: basic features
- Paid: $10/month starting price
- Credit card accepted
- May require VPN from China

## Alternatives
- Hex
- Mode
- Looker

## Who it's for
Data analysts, business users who want quick data exploration without SQL.
