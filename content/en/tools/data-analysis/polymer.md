# Polymer

Turn your data into interactive dashboards. Upload your dataset and drag-and-drop your way to beautiful visualizations and dashboards. No coding required, no SQL needed — self-service analytics for business users.

## Strengths
- Drag-and-drop dashboard creation
- AI auto-discovers data insights
- Multiple data source support
- Shareable and collaborative

## Pricing & Access
- Free: basic features, 1 user
- Paid: $20/month starting price
- Credit card accepted
- May require VPN from China

## Alternatives
- Tableau
- Looker
- Power BI

## Who it's for
Business users and managers who need data dashboards without coding.
