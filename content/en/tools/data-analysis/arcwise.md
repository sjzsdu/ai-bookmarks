Arcwise bolts an AI analyst onto Google Sheets: no exports, no new tools — ask 'which channel grew fastest this month?' and it locates the data, runs the analysis, and answers. Formulas and cleaning happen in natural language too. For Sheets-native operators, it's the lowest-friction AI analytics entry.

## Strengths
- In-sheet AI Q&A and insights
- Natural-language formula generation
- Data-cleaning suggestions and execution
- Narrative reports from sheet data

## Pricing & access
Free quota; from ~$20/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
None (Sheets-bound); WPS AI covers similar ground domestically.

## Bottom line
An AI booster for Sheets users — ecosystem-bound but zero friction.
