# Tongyi Tingwu

Alibaba DAMO Academy's meeting analysis tool. Upload a recording and it auto-transcribes to text, generates meeting minutes, and extracts action items. Excellent Chinese transcription accuracy, completely free.

## Strengths
- Auto-transcription of meeting recordings
- Generates minutes and action items
- Supports Chinese-English mixed transcription
- Searchable history and sharing

## Pricing & Access
- Completely free
- Direct access from China, fast speed
- No VPN needed
- Web and mini-program available

## Alternatives
- Feishu Minutes
- Tencent Meeting recording
- iFlytekrec

## Who it's for
Product managers and team leads who have lots of meetings and need organized notes.
