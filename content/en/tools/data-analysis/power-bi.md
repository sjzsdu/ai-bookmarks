Power BI's play is 'good enough + cheap + ecosystem': free desktop for individuals, $10/month Pro for org-wide sharing — table scraps compared to Tableau. Visuals trail slightly but suffice, and Copilot now writes reports from prompts. For Microsoft shops, there's essentially no competitor.

## Strengths
- Data modeling (DAX) and interactive visuals
- Copilot report generation
- Excel, Azure, Teams integration
- Org publishing, subscriptions, security

## Pricing & access
Desktop free; Pro $10/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
A China-operated edition exists; FineBI is the local rival.

## Bottom line
The value king of enterprise BI.
