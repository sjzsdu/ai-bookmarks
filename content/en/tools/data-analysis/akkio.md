# Akkio

Akkio is a no-code AI data platform for business teams. Connect or upload data, explore metrics in natural language, build predictive models, and turn the results into shareable reports.

## Strengths
- No-code data preparation, analysis, and visualization
- Natural-language questions and automated reports
- Classification, forecasting, and lead scoring
- Connections to spreadsheets, databases, and business tools

## Pricing & access
Trials or demos are generally available. Production plans vary by features, users, and data volume; request current pricing from Akkio.

## Who it's for
Marketing, sales, operations, and consulting teams that need predictive analytics without a dedicated data-science team.
