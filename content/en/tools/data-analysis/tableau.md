Tableau defined drag-and-drop visualization: pull fields into rows and columns, watch charts respond — still the expressiveness ceiling. Under Salesforce it gained AI: Tableau Pulse monitors business metrics and narrates anomalies in natural language. Enterprise deployment, governance, and permissions are complete.

## Strengths
- Interactive drag-and-drop visualization
- Tableau Pulse for AI metric monitoring
- Data preparation (Tableau Prep)
- Enterprise publishing and permissions

## Pricing & access
~$75/month Creator; free for students and teachers. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
FineBI and Guandata domestically with smoother local support.

## Bottom line
The benchmark and the expensive option — also the best one to learn.
