Power Automate merges Microsoft's cloud automation with RPA: connectors wire Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, and 1000+ SaaS apps, while Power Automate Desktop records and replays human operations — free with Windows 11. For Microsoft-shop enterprises, it's the default answer to office automation.

## Strengths
- 1000+ cloud connectors
- Desktop RPA with record/replay/scripts
- AI Builder for documents, predictions, sentiment
- Unified with M365 security and governance

## Pricing & access

Desktop free; cloud from ~$15/user/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Yingdao RPA (desktop) and Feishu/DingTalk flows (cloud) domestically.

## Bottom line

Utilities-grade automation inside the Microsoft world; weaker outside it.
