# Make

Visual automation that uses flowcharts instead of linear steps — more flexible and often cheaper than Zapier.

## Strengths
- Flowchart-style visual editor for clear logic
- Complex branching, loops, and error handling
- AI modules for calling various LLMs
- Better pricing than Zapier for similar usage

## Pricing & access
Free tier: 1,000 operations/month. Paid from $9/month. Global access.

## Alternatives
Zapier (more integrations), n8n (self-hosted).

## Who it's for
Automation enthusiasts who need complex workflows and want better value.
