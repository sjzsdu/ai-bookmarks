Activepieces pairs 'Zapier's experience' with 'open-source autonomy': self-host for full data ownership, or use the cloud at a fraction of Zapier's price. AI steps are first-class — calling OpenAI inside a flow is drag-and-drop. Good for teams that want automation without SaaS lock-in.

## Strengths
- 400+ connectors, modern UI
- Native AI steps (OpenAI/Anthropic etc.)
- MIT-licensed, Docker self-hosting
- Webhooks, schedules, loops — full logic

## Pricing & access

Self-host free; cloud from ~$25/month. Proxy required in China.

## Compared
n8n (deeper, steeper), Zapier (easiest, priciest); Activepieces sits between.

## Bottom line

The friendliest on-ramp in open-source automation.
