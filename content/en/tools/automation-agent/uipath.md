UiPath defined RPA: software robots mimicking human desktop actions — clicking buttons, filling forms, moving data across systems. When a process touches legacy software without APIs, RPA is the only answer. It has since woven LLMs into document understanding and decisioning, giving the old tree new shoots.

## Strengths
- Desktop RPA across arbitrary software
- Document Understanding for invoices/forms
- AI Center for model-driven orchestration
- Enterprise governance, audit, scheduling

## Pricing & access

Free community edition; enterprise on request. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Yingdao RPA and Laiye domestically, with local compliance and pricing advantages.

## Bottom line

Heavy machinery for enterprise automation; individuals should stay on the community edition.
