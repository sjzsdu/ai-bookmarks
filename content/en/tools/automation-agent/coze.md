# Coze

ByteDance's no-code AI agent platform — drag-and-drop bots published to many channels.

## Strengths
- Bot orchestration, plugin marketplace, knowledge base, workflows
- Direct access in China with Doubao model, great Chinese ecosystem
- Multi-channel: WeChat, Feishu, web
- Fast setup, no technical skills needed

## Pricing & access
Free, direct access in China, no VPN. Phone registration.

## Alternatives
Dify (self-hostable), n8n (general automation).

## Who it's for
Ops, customer service, product managers, and teams prototyping AI cheaply.
