# n8n

Open-source workflow automation that connects systems with nodes — and can drop LLMs into the flow.

## Strengths
- Visual automation with 400+ integrations
- HTTP/DB/AI nodes for flexible combinations
- Schedule and event triggers
- Open source, self-hostable, data stays on your server

## Pricing & access
Open source, free to self-host. Cloud version pay-as-you-go in EUR.

## Alternatives
Huginn (open source), Activepieces (open source automation).

## Who it's for
Ops, IT, and developers embedding AI into internal business processes.
