# Microsoft Azure TTS

Microsoft's Cognitive Services speech synthesis: multilingual neural voices, enterprise-stable and customizable.

## Why enterprises choose it
Azure China (21Vianet) enables compliant in-region calls with RMB billing; neural voices are natural; custom voices available (on request); SLA and concurrency for production.

## Capabilities
Multilingual TTS, emotional speech, real-time/batch synthesis, fine SSML control.

## Who it's for
Apps, call centers, navigation, audiobook platforms needing stable voice output.
