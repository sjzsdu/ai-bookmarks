Gauth (formerly Gauthmath) is ByteDance's photo-solving app for students worldwide: photograph a problem and get step-by-step solutions, with solid coverage from algebra to calculus plus physics and chemistry. Same lane as China's Zuoyebang but stronger internationally. Use it to understand steps, not to copy answers.

## Strengths
- Photo recognition with worked steps
- All math levels plus physics/chemistry
- Multilingual interface and problem support
- 1v1 human tutors (membership)

## Pricing & access

Free basics; membership for unlimited use. Direct access.

## Compared
Zuoyebang and Xiaoyuan (deeper Chinese curriculum); Gauth wins on internationalization and AI steps.

## Bottom line

The most AI-forward of the photo-solving apps.
