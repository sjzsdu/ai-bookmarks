# Julius AI

Data analysis without coding — upload files, describe what you want, get charts and insights.

## Strengths
- Upload CSV/Excel/JSON for analysis
- Natural language analysis requests
- Auto-generated charts and visualizations
- SQL query support

## Pricing & access
Free tier with limited analyses. Pro at $20/month. Mostly accessible globally.

## Alternatives
Google Colab (free, but requires coding), ChatGPT Code Interpreter (similar concept).

## Who it's for
Non-technical product managers, ops people, and students who need data analysis.
