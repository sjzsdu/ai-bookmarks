# Duolingo

The most popular language learning app — gamified AI that makes daily practice stick.

## Strengths
- Gamified design with streaks and XP
- AI adapts difficulty to your level
- Speaking, listening, reading, and writing practice
- 40+ languages available

## Pricing & access
Free with ads and daily limits. Super at $7/month. Available globally, works in China.

## Alternatives
Babbel (more structured), HelloTalk (conversation practice).

## Who it's for
Anyone wanting to learn a language in short daily sessions — especially beginners.
