# fast.ai

Jeremy Howard's free deep learning course — practical-first, you'll train models from lesson one.

## Strengths
- Go from zero to training your own models
- Completely free
- fastai library simplifies PyTorch
- Active community forum

## Pricing & access
Completely free. Videos on YouTube, course site has notes and code.

## Alternatives
Andrew Ng's Coursera course (more structured), Stanford CS231n (more academic).

## Who it's for
Developers and researchers with Python basics who want to learn deep learning practically.
