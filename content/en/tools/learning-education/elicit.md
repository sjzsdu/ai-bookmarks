# Elicit

AI research assistant that does the tedious part of literature review — extract, compare, and summarize.

## Strengths
- Auto-extracts methods, findings, sample sizes from papers
- Literature review synthesis across multiple papers
- Paper summaries and comparisons
- Batch analysis support

## Pricing & access
Free tier with monthly credits. Pro at $10/month. Global access.

## Alternatives
Semantic Scholar (free, less extraction), Consensus (AI research search).

## Who it's for
Graduate students, PhDs, and researchers doing literature reviews.
