Brilliant courses contain no filler video: they're interactive experiments — drag a slider to morph a function, flip switches to grasp logic gates, tune parameters to train a network. It teaches why, not just how, rebuilding STEM intuition. The 15-minute daily rhythm makes consistency easy.

## Strengths
- Thousands of interactive lessons and puzzles
- Math, logic, CS, data science
- Visual experiments over derivations
- Gamified daily practice

## Pricing & access

Free daily tier; ~$24.99/month (annual discounts). Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
3Blue1Brown (free videos, intuition), Khan Academy (free, systematic).

## Bottom line

Paid but justified — nothing better for building mathematical intuition.
