Consensus answers 'what does the research say about X': type a research question and it searches vast paper indexes, summarizes findings, and shows a consensus meter — how many studies agree, disagree, and how strong the evidence is. It compresses days of literature review into minutes, fully traceable.

## Strengths
- AI search over 200M+ papers
- Consensus indicators (support/contradict/mixed)
- Findings with real citations
- Quality filters (sample size, journal grade)

## Pricing & access

Free tier; paid from ~$8.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
No true domestic equivalent; general chatbots lack traceability.

## Bottom line

The literature layer for evidence-based work — a gift for students and early researchers.
