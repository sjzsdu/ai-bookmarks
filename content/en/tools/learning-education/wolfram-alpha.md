Wolfram Alpha is a 'computational knowledge engine': it refuses vague questions, but feed it an integral, an equation, or a unit conversion and it returns exact results — not an LLM's 'probably'. Physical constants, chemical elements, and statistical facts come from authoritative data. For STEM learning it complements chat AI rather than competing.

## Strengths
- Exact math: calculus, equations, matrices
- Physics, chemistry, engineering computation
- Factual scientific and economic data
- Step display (Pro)

## Pricing & access

Free basics; Pro ~$5/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Symbolab for math steps; no real equivalent for symbolic computation.

## Bottom line

STEM infrastructure — best alongside, not instead of, chat AI.
