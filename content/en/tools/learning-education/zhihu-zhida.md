Zhida pipes Zhihu's content pool into AI Q&A: experiential and method questions ('how to get into X', 'what X industry is really like') often surface summaries built from genuine practitioners' answers, with cited sources. As a learning entry point, it carries more human grounding than generic chatbots.

## Strengths
- Zhihu community content plus web search
- Answers traceable to creators
- Short/deep answer modes
- Free and friction-free
