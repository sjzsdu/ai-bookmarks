# Coursera

The largest MOOC platform — university-quality AI courses from Stanford, DeepLearning.AI, and Google.

## Strengths
- University courses (Stanford CS229, DeepLearning.AI series)
- Verified certificates for LinkedIn
- Hands-on assignments and projects
- Chinese courses and subtitles available

## Pricing & access
Audit most courses free. Certificates and specializations from $49/month. Global access.

## Alternatives
edX (similar quality), fast.ai (free, more practical).

## Who it's for
People wanting structured AI/ML learning, especially career changers needing certificates.
