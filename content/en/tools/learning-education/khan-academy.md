# Khan Academy

The most generous education platform — all courses free, AI tutor Khanmigo for one-on-one help.

## Strengths
- Comprehensive math, science, computing, and humanities courses
- Khanmigo AI tutor with Socratic questioning
- Instant feedback on exercises and quizzes
- Learning progress tracking

## Pricing & access
Courses are completely free. Khanmigo AI tutor is $44/year. Globally accessible.

## Alternatives
Coursera (more structured), Brilliant (interactive STEM).

## Who it's for
Students and self-learners, especially those building foundations in math and science.
