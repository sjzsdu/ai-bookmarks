Speak removes the biggest barrier to speaking practice — embarrassment — by putting AI on the other side: make mistakes freely while it corrects pronunciation and suggests more natural phrasing in real time. Courses are scenario-based (coffee shops, meetings, interviews), training real conversation instead of quizzes.

## Strengths
- AI voice chat with live pronunciation feedback
- Scenario courses (daily/work/travel)
- More-idiomatic phrasing suggestions
- Progress and weakness tracking

## Pricing & access

From ~$12.99/month. Proxy required in China.

## Alternatives
Duolingo (gamified, weaker speaking), Liulishuo (domestic); Speak leads on immersion.

## Bottom line

The benchmark for AI speaking practice — worth paying for if you need to open your mouth.
